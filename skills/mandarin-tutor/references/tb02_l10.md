# 第十課 歡迎到我家來包餃子

*Dì shí kè — Huānyíng dào wǒ jiā lái bāo jiǎozi*

Come to My Place to Make Dumplings

> **Note on pinyin:** Textbook 2 prints no romanization — its text blocks are Traditional + Simplified + English only. The pinyin below is supplied editorially so the lesson can be read aloud; it is not printed in the source.

## 學習目標 Learning Objectives

**Topic: 交際 Socializing**

- Learning to explain how to make a dish.
- Learning to talk about ingredients.
- Learning to introduce the cuisines found in my country.
- Learning to express surprise.

**張老師：** 歡迎，歡迎，大家請進，不要脫鞋。月美，麻煩妳關門。

*Zhāng lǎoshī: Huānyíng, huānyíng, dàjiā qǐng jìn, bú yào tuō xié. Yuèměi, máfan nǐ guān mén.*

Teacher Zhang: Welcome! Welcome! Everybody come on in. Don't take your shoes off. Yuemei, would you please close the door?

**月美：** 謝謝老師請我們來包餃子，我也帶了我們越南的春捲。

*Yuèměi: Xièxie lǎoshī qǐng wǒmen lái bāo jiǎozi, wǒ yě dài le wǒmen Yuènán de chūnjuǎn.*

Yuemei: Thank you, Teacher Zhang, for having us over to make dumplings. I brought some Vietnamese spring rolls.

**東健：** 張老師，這是我做的韓國泡菜。

*Dōngjiàn: Zhāng lǎoshī, zhè shì wǒ zuò de Hánguó pàocài.*

Dongjian: Teacher Zhang, this is some kimchi that I made.

**張老師：** 看不出來你還會做泡菜！馬丁、高美玲，你們也帶東西來了啊？

*Zhāng lǎoshī: Kàn bù chūlái nǐ hái huì zuò pàocài! Mǎding, Gāo Měilíng, nǐmen yě dài dōngxi lái le a?*

Teacher Zhang: I couldn't tell by looking at you that you could make kimchi. Martin and Gao Meiling, you brought some things also?

**馬丁：** 我們剛剛去超市買了水果跟飲料。老師，你們家的飯廳真大。

*Mǎding: Wǒmen gānggāng qù chāoshì mǎi le shuǐguǒ gēn yǐnliào. Lǎoshī, nǐmen jiā de fàntīng zhēn dà.*

Martin: We stopped by the supermarket and got some fruit and drinks just now. Teacher Zhang, your dining room is huge.

**美玲：** 老師家的飯廳應該坐得下十個人吧？

*Měilíng: Lǎoshī jiā de fàntīng yīnggāi zuò de xià shí ge rén ba?*

Meiling: I gather the dining room should be able to sit ten.

**張老師：** 坐得下，所以請你們幾個人一起來吃飯沒問題。

*Zhāng lǎoshī: Zuò de xià, suǒyǐ qǐng nǐmen jǐ ge rén yìqǐ lái chīfàn méi wèntí.*

Teacher Zhang: Absolutely. So inviting you few over to eat isn't a problem.

**東健：** 老師，我們要幫忙準備什麼？

*Dōngjiàn: Lǎoshī, wǒmen yào bāngmáng zhǔnbèi shénme?*

Dongjian: Teacher Zhang, can we help get anything ready?

**張老師：** 我把包餃子的東西都準備好了，你們去把手洗一洗，我們就開始包吧！

*Zhāng lǎoshī: Wǒ bǎ bāo jiǎozi de dōngxi dōu zhǔnbèi hǎo le, nǐmen qù bǎ shǒu xǐ yi xǐ, wǒmen jiù kāishǐ bāo ba!*

Teacher Zhang: I have everything needed to wrap the dumplings ready. You guys go ahead and go wash your hands and we can start wrapping.

**美玲：** 這是我第一次包餃子，你們包過嗎？

*Měilíng: Zhè shì wǒ dì yī cì bāo jiǎozi, nǐmen bāo guò ma?*

Meiling: This is my first time making dumplings. Have any of you done it before?

**月美：** 我跟馬丁以前上過做菜的課，包過水餃，對不對？

*Yuèměi: Wǒ gēn Mǎding yǐqián shàng guò zuòcài de kè, bāo guò shuǐjiǎo, duì bú duì?*

Yuemei: Martin and I took cooking classes before and we made dumplings, right?

**馬丁：** 包過，可是我包的水餃一煮就壞了。

*Mǎding: Bāo guò, kěshì wǒ bāo de shuǐjiǎo yì zhǔ jiù huài le.*

Martin: Yes, we did. But the dumplings I made went bad as soon as they were put in the pot.

**張老師：** 不是壞了，是煮破了。如果你們沒把餃子包好，一煮就破了。

*Zhāng lǎoshī: Bú shì huài le, shì zhǔ pò le. Rúguǒ nǐmen méi bǎ jiǎozi bāo hǎo, yì zhǔ jiù pò le.*

Teacher Zhang: They didn't "go bad". They split open. If you don't wrap them well, they split as soon as they are put in.

**東健：** 老師，您看我這麼包，對嗎？

*Dōngjiàn: Lǎoshī, nín kàn wǒ zhème bāo, duì ma?*

Dongjian: Teacher Zhang, am I doing this right?

**張老師：** 很好，不過你還可以多放一點餡兒，如果扁扁的，看起來就不像餃子了。

*Zhāng lǎoshī: Hěn hǎo, búguò nǐ hái kěyǐ duō fàng yìdiǎn xiànr, rúguǒ biǎnbiǎn de, kàn qǐlái jiù bú xiàng jiǎozi le.*

Teacher Zhang: Very good. But you can still put a bit more filling in them. If they are too flat, they don't look like proper dumplings.

**月美：** 老師，我們五個人要包多少餃子？

*Yuèměi: Lǎoshī, wǒmen wǔ ge rén yào bāo duōshao jiǎozi?*

Yuemei: Teacher Zhang, how many do the five of us need to make?

**張老師：** 我準備的材料大概可以包一百多個，再做幾道菜，還有一大碗酸辣湯，應該夠了。大家一定都吃得飽。

*Zhāng lǎoshī: Wǒ zhǔnbèi de cáiliào dàgài kěyǐ bāo yì bǎi duō ge, zài zuò jǐ dào cài, hái yǒu yí dà wǎn suānlà tāng, yīnggāi gòu le. Dàjiā yídìng dōu chī de bǎo.*

Teacher Zhang: We should be able to make over 100 with the ingredients I prepared. I will make a few more dishes and a big bowl of hot and sour soup. That should be enough for everybody to eat their fill.

**美玲：** 馬丁，你來以前不是一直說餓嗎？你能吃幾個餃子？

*Měilíng: Mǎding, nǐ lái yǐqián bú shì yìzhí shuō è ma? Nǐ néng chī jǐ ge jiǎozi?*

Meiling: Martin, didn't you keep saying you were hungry before you came? How many dumplings can you eat?

**馬丁：** 我想我吃得下二十個，要是妳吃不完，我可以幫妳吃。

*Mǎding: Wǒ xiǎng wǒ chī de xià èrshí ge, yàoshi nǐ chī bù wán, wǒ kěyǐ bāng nǐ chī.*

Martin: I think I can eat 20. If you can't finish yours, I can help you with them.

**張老師：** 大家最好能把餃子都吃光。你們包，我去把鍋子洗了，就要準備煮餃子了。

*Zhāng lǎoshī: Dàjiā zuìhǎo néng bǎ jiǎozi dōu chī guāng. Nǐmen bāo, wǒ qù bǎ guōzi xǐ le, jiù yào zhǔnbèi zhǔ jiǎozi le.*

Teacher Zhang: It would be great if everyone could finish all of the dumplings. You all wrap. I'll go wash the wok and we can get ready to cook the dumplings.

**月美：** 老師，我幫您把筷子和碗放在桌上。

*Yuèměi: Lǎoshī, wǒ bāng nín bǎ kuàizi hé wǎn fàng zài zhuō shàng.*

Yuemei: Teacher Zhang, I'll put the chopsticks and bowls on the table for you.

## 文法 Grammar

### I. To Figure Out with the Complement 出來 chūlái

**Function:** When 出來 serves as a complement to verbs of cognition and senses, it refers to a result of 'being figured out', ending a cognitive processing.

- 這張三十年以前的照片，你得看久一點，才能看出來是誰。
- 馬安同聽出來那個人說的是台灣話。
- 這個旅行路線的建議是他想出來的。

**Structures:** 出來 can be used in all the four complement structures, i.e., V 出來, 沒 V 出來, V 得出來, and V 不出來.

- 陳月美喝得出來這杯是烏龍茶。
- 田中誠一寫得出來這個中國字。
- 王開文吃不出來那種食物是什麼材料做的。
- 李東健看不出來那個人是台灣人還是日本人。

**Negation:**

- 他沒吃出來媽媽在水餃裡放了什麼菜。
- 高美玲沒喝出來飲料裡面有什麼水果。
- 考試的時候，馬安同沒想出來那個複雜的漢字怎麼寫。

**Questions:**

- 你聽不聽得出來說話的那個人是美國人？
  （＝ 你聽得出來聽不出來說話的那個人是美國人？）
- 你看得出來看不出來老師說的那個字？
- 我們上個月花了多少生活費，你算出來了沒有？

**Usage:** If the object is short, it can appear between 出 and 來.

- 是他想出那個建議來的。
- 你寫不寫得出這個字來？

Also, when the pattern is used with non-cognitive verbs, 出來 simply refers to the ability of accomplishing something, 'able to', e.g.

- 你們公司是不是有問題？這個月薪水還沒發出來！
- 如果你沒說出來，沒有人會知道你幫過他。
- 白如玉拍得出來那麼好看的照片，可是我拍不出來。

### II. Enough Space to Accommodate with Verb Complement 下 xià

**Function:** The construction V + 得／不 + 下 indicates whether there's enough space to accommodate something.

- 這個車子坐得下九個人。
- 李明華喝得下大杯咖啡。
- 二十個餃子，陳月美都吃得下。

**Structures:** *(the textbook prints this heading without a description line)*

**Negation:**

- 背包太小了，裝不下這個電腦。
- 這個資源回收桶放不下這麼多塑膠瓶子。
- 這間教室坐不下那麼多學生。

**Questions:**

- 這個房間放得下放不下一張大床？
  （＝ 這個房間放不放得下一張大床？）
- 這間教室坐得下坐不下五十個學生？
- 你的隨身碟存不存得下這些照片？

**Usage:** The verb complements 下 and 了 can combine with the same verbs, but the differences in meaning are clear.

我的車坐不下五個人 vs. 我的車坐不了五個人. The former refers to 'not enough space', while the latter 'inability to seat, incapable of seating'.

### III. Disposal Construction 把 bǎ with Verb Reduplication 把 bǎ + V(一)V

**Function:** The V(一)V construction was introduced in Lesson 6 in Vol. 1. The VV pattern softens the action and also makes it brief. This pattern is often combined with the 把 construction in making requests or orders, indicating 'doing something briefly to a noun'. Fuller meanings of such sentences should be observed by referring to the English translations on page 257.

- 你把那本書看一看，再告訴我好不好看。
- 馬安同把提出來的錢算一算，再去付學費。
- 請你把學校上課的情形跟新同學說一說。

**Structures:** The verb in 'V-V' has to be monosyllabic.

**Negation:**

- 你現在不先把餃子包一包，明天就沒空包餃子了。
- 他做了菜，沒把鍋子洗一洗，就煮牛肉湯了。
- 你不先把身體動一動，等一下打太極拳一定很累。

**Questions:**

- 你有空的時候，能不能請你把垃圾倒一倒？

### IV. Disposal Construction 把 bǎ with Resultative Complements

**Function:** In the 把 construction, the complement after the verb may indicate a result of the action. The complements come in 2 forms, V + C or V + 得 + C.

- 媽媽不小心把餃子煮破了。
- 請你把白菜洗乾淨，等一下就要用了。
- 他把水餃都包得好難看。

**Structures:** The complement indicating the result of action in this construction can be a state verb, a process verb or a phrase/clause following 得.

- 你這麼洗，會把鍋子洗壞。
- 他第一次做泡菜，把泡菜做得很難吃。
- 你趕快把窗戶關好，我們要出去了。

**Negation:**

- 別把菜吃光了，要留一點給妹妹。
- 如果我沒把碗洗乾淨，老闆會要我再洗一次。
- 你不把話說完，他不知道你想做什麼。

**Questions:**

- 你把湯喝完了沒有？
- 他是不是把餃子煮破了？
- 你是不是把小籠包都吃光了？

**Usage:** As can be seen in the examples above, 了 is often added at the end of a 把 sentence to express change of state, which is highly compatible with the meaning of resultative complements.

### V. 趁 chèn — Seize the Moment

**Function:** The preposition 趁 refers to seizing an opportune moment for the implementation of an action.

- 林老師趁這個週末沒事，到東部的海邊走走。
- 馬安同趁老師還沒來上課，出去買了一杯咖啡。
- 趁天氣好，李東健和高美玲去河邊的公園騎腳踏車。

**Structures:** 趁 can introduce a noun phrase, a verb phrase, or a clause. The '趁 + …' construction can appear either before or after the subject.

**Questions:**

- 我們要不要趁這個週末，搭捷運去看電腦展？
- 你不趁年輕的時候打打工，怎麼會了解工作的情形呢？
- 你是不是應該趁老師還沒來，趕快把作業寫完？

**Usage:** 趁 and 趁著 mean practically the same thing. Either 趁 or 趁著 can be used in the following example, 我們趁（著）天氣好，去山上走一走 'We took a walk in the mountain while the weather was nice'. 趁 alone is more colloquial and has a higher frequency of occurrence.

## 中華文化點滴 Bits of Chinese Culture

### Where Can You Sample All the Best Cuisines of China? Taiwan

*[printed in English in the textbook]*

Traditional Taiwanese cuisine traces its culinary lineage to China's Fujian region. Today, however, Taiwan offers regional cooking from all over China as well as cuisines from many other countries of the world. The Book of Rites, a Confucian classic, states that "Food is the god of the people" (民以食為天) highlighting the primacy of eating in human life. "When eating you're as grand as the emperor" (吃飯皇帝大) is a pithy Taiwanese saying that sums up food's unrivaled importance in Chinese culture.

How is it that cuisines from all regions of China are available in Taiwan today? When the Nationalist government relocated to Taiwan in 1949, military and administrative personnel from all over China settled in cities, towns, and "military dependents' villages" in every part of the island. With them came regional cooking styles that they shared with local Taiwanese and friends from other areas of China. At the time many augmented slender incomes and retirement pensions by opening small eateries that specialized in dishes from their native provinces. Chinese love to eat and are ever eager to sample cuisines different from those of their own regions. From that time on Taiwanese cuisine began to evolve — northern Chinese, who had traditionally eaten a wheat-based diet, now began to eat rice dishes; southern Chinese, whose staple food had always been rice, were now dining on wheat-flour noodles and other dishes from the north. Regional dishes from every part of China became popular. What's more, these were combined with native Taiwanese ingredients and cooking methods to produce unique new flavors. Beef noodle soup (牛肉麵) is the best example of this kind of culinary innovation — many Taiwanese think it's a Sichuan (四川) dish, but the beef noodle soup dishes served in Sichuan province today taste nothing like the Taiwanese version. Taiwan-style beef noodle soup originated in an Air Force military dependents' village in Kaohsiung, where soldiers from the Sichuan region recreated their favorite native dishes with ingredients available in Taiwan.
