## 對話 Dialogue 05-01

> （馬安同和雲真在夜市相遇，談到博士畢業生賣雞排、啃老族等社會現象）

**馬安同：** 雲真，妳也受到連續劇的影響來排隊買雞排嗎？

*Mǎ Āntóng: Yúnzhēn, nǐ yě shòudào liánxùjù de yǐngxiǎng lái páiduì mǎi jīpái ma?*

**李雲真：** 我哪裡有時間看連續劇？我不是來買雞排的，而是為了校刊來採訪老闆的。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Wǒ nǎlǐ yǒu shíjiān kàn liánxùjù? Wǒ búshì lái mǎi jīpái de, érsìshì wèile xiàokān lái cǎifǎng lǎobǎn de.*

**馬安同：** 這個賣雞排的老闆很有名嗎？

*Mǎ Āntóng: Zhège mài jīpái de lǎobǎn yǒu hěn míng ma?*

**李雲真：** 你沒聽說過嗎？他是我們學校的校友，去年剛得到博士學位。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Nǐ méi tīngshuō guo ma? Tā shì wǒmen xuéxiào de xiáoyǒu, qùnián gāng dédào bóshì xuéwèi.*

**馬安同：** 我沒聽錯吧？既然有那麼高的學歷，為什麼要在夜市裡賣雞排呢？

*Mǎ Āntóng: Wǒ méi tīngcuò ba? Jìrán yǒu nàme gāo de xuélì, wèishénme yào zài yèshì lǐ mǎi jīpái ne?*

**李雲真：** 现在博士去考清潔隊員、碩士去當司機和學士擺地攤的現象在台灣越來越普遍，已經不稀奇了。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Xiànzài bóshì qù kǎo qīngjié duìyuán, shuòshì qù dāng sījī hé xuéshì bǎi dìtān de xiànxiàng zài Táiwān yuèlái yuè pǔbiàn, yǐjīng bù xīqí le.*

**馬安同：** 賣雞排並不丟臉，不偷不搶，靠自己賺錢，很好啊！

*Mǎ Āntóng: Mài jīpái bìngbú diūliǎn, bú tōu bú qiǎng, kào zìjǐ zhùan qián, hěn hǎo a!*

**李雲真：** 我也這麼認為，但也聽到不少負面的看法，批評這種人浪費國家的教育資源。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Wǒ yě zhème rènwéi, dàn yě tīngdao búshǎo fùmiàn de kànfǎ, pīpíng zhè zhǒng rén làngfèi guójiā de jiàoyù zīyuán.*

**馬安同：** 這個我可以了解，畢竟念到博士不容易，應該可以為國家貢獻更多才對。

*Mǎ Āntóng: Zhège wǒ kěyǐ liǎojiě, bìjìng niàn dào bóshì bù róngyì, yīnggāi kěyǐ wèi guójiā gòngxiàn gèng duō cái duì.*

**李雲真：** 這的確是個很嚴重的問題。很多人盲目升學，甚至有些學生因為要逃避就業而延後畢業的時間。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Zhè dequè shì ge hěn yánzhòng de wèntí. Hěn duō rén mángmù shēngxué, shènzhì yǒuxiē xuéshēng yīnwèi yào táobì jiùyè ér yánhòu bìyè de shíjiān.*

**馬安同：** 在我的國家並沒有這個問題，因為大多數人都念不起大學。

*Mǎ Āntóng: Zài wǒ de guójiā bìngméiyǒu zhège wèntí, yīnwèi dàduōshù rén dū niàn bù qǐ dàxué.*

**李雲真：** 不過話說回來，能自食其力的人比那些「靠爸族」好多了。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Búguò huàshuō huílái, néng zìshí qílì de rén bǐ nàxiē "kào bàzú" hǎo duōle.*

**馬安同：** 所謂「靠爸族」是指靠父母生活嗎？

*Mǎ Āntóng: Suǒwèi "kào bàzú" shì zhǐ kào fùmǔ shēnghuó ma?*

**李雲真：** 大概的意思是：自己沒什麼能力，這些人之所以成功是因為父母的關係。另外還有一種人叫「啃老族」，就是自己沒有能力獨立生活，還住在家里靠父母的人。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Dàgài de yìsi shì: zìjǐ méi shénme nénglì, zhèxiē rén zhīsuǒyǐ chénggōng shì yīnwèi fùmǔ de guānxì. Lìngwài hái yǒu yī zhǒng rén jiào "kěnlǎozú", jiùshì zìjǐ méiyǒu nénglì dúlì shēnghuó, hái zhù zài jiālǐ kào fùmǔ de rén.*

**馬安同：** 我的國家現在也有不少這種人。我沒有有錢的父母，得趕快去圖書館看書了，畢業後我得靠自己啊！

*Mǎ Āntóng: Wǒ de guójiā xiànzài yě búshǎo zhè zhǒng rén. Wǒ méiyǒu yǒuqián de fùmǔ, děi gǎn kuài qù túshūguǎn kàn shū le, bìyè hòu wǒ děi kào zìjǐ a!*

**李雲真：** 我也該去採訪了，免得等一下老闆收攤，我就採訪不成了。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Wǒ yě gāi qù cǎifǎng le, miǎndé děng yīxià lǎobǎn shōutān, wǒ jiù cǎifǎng bùchéng le.*

**馬安同：** 那麼，希望妳採訪順利，我等著看下一期的校刊。

*Mǎ Āntóng: Nàme, xīwàng nǐ cǎifǎng shùnli, wǒ děngzhe kàn xiàyīqì de xiàokān.*

**李雲真：** 謝謝，明天見。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Xièxie, míngtiān jiàn.*

**馬安同：** 對了，採訪完了以後，別忘了買一些「博士雞排」吃吃看。

*Mǎ Āntóng: Duìle, cǎifǎng wánle yǐhòu, bié wàngle mǎi yīxiē "bóshì jīpái" chīchī kàn.*

**李雲真：** 希望我吃了以後，也可以早一點拿到博士學位。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Xīwàng wǒ chīle yǐhòu, yě kěyǐ zǎo yīdiǎn ná dào bóshì xuéwèi.*

**李云真：** 谢谢，明天见。

**马安同：** 对了，采访完了以后，别忘了买一些「博士鸡排」吃吃看。

**李云真：** 希望我吃了以后，也可以早一点拿到博士学位。

### English

**Ma Antong:** Yunzhen, are you also lining up to buy chicken cutlets because of that TV drama?

**Li Yunzhen:** I don't have time to watch dramas. I'm not here for the chicken cutlets — I'm here for the school newspaper to interview the boss.

**Ma Antong:** This chicken cutlet boss is famous?

**Li Yunzhen:** You haven't heard of him? He's our school's alumnus. He just got his PhD last year.

**Ma Antong:** No way? With that level of education, why sell chicken cutlets in a night market?

**Li Yunzhen:** These days it's common — PhDs taking cleaning jobs, masters driving taxis, bachelor's degree holders running street stalls. Nothing surprising anymore.

**Ma Antong:** Selling chicken cutlets isn't shameful — no stealing, no robbing, earning your own money. Good for her!

**Li Yunzhen:** I agree. But I've also heard negative comments — people say she's wasting the nation's educational resources.

**Ma Antong:** I get that. After all, getting a PhD isn't easy. She should be able to contribute more to the country.

**Li Yunzhen:** That's a serious problem. Many people blindly pursue higher education. Some even postpone graduation just to avoid entering the job market.

**Ma Antong:** In my country there's no such problem — most people can't even afford college.

**Li Yunzhen:** But then again, people who stand on their own two feet are better off than those "parasites" who live off their parents.

**Ma Antong:** So "parasites" means people who live off their parents?

**Li Yunzhen:** Something like that — people with no ability of their own who succeed only because of their parents' connections. And then there's the "knail old clan" — people who can't live independently and still stay home depending on their parents.

**Ma Antong:** My country has plenty of those too. I have no rich parents, so I'd better rush to the library and study. After graduation I'll have to rely on myself!

**Li Yunzhen:** I should go interview too, or the boss will close up and I'll miss my chance.

**Ma Antong:** Well, good luck with the interview. I'll wait for the next issue of the school paper.

**Li Yunzhen:** Thanks, see you tomorrow.

**Ma Antong:** Oh, and after you're done, don't forget to buy some "PhD chicken cutlets" to try.

**Li Yunzhen:** I hope after eating them, I can get my PhD soon too.

## 短文 Reading 05-03

### 別放棄夢想

在台灣生活了一段時間以後，馬安同覺得台灣的物價比他剛來的時候上漲了不少。有越來越多的台灣人抱怨這種低所得、高物價的情況，什麼都漲了，就是薪水沒漲。一般大學剛畢業的年輕人，不是找不到工作，就是每個月的薪水只能勉強生活。不少人辛苦工作了很多年，卻根本存不了什麼錢，更別說有能力買房子了。有些人還成立了「無殼蝸牛」組織，抗議台灣地價和房價高漲，造成貧富不均，人民買不起房子。

根據台灣政府的統計，每年的失業率大概在百分之三到五左右。雖然跟其他國家比起來，台灣的失業率並不特別高，但人民卻覺得生活苦哈哈的，很多人都是「窮忙族」。這種現象一方面受到全球經濟不景氣的影響，另一方面是因為政府所施行的經濟政策也有問題。大多數人的薪資多年未調高不說，還有不少人被裁員。在台灣找不到工作的，不得不去中國大陸或別的國家另謀發展。

馬安同今年在台灣過年時，他觀察到一些有趣的現象。不少台灣人恨不得能一夜致富，大家喜歡買彩券，希望能中獎，常做發財夢。另外還有一個現象就是每個人都想當老闆，不想看別人的臉色，所以在台灣有不少只有三、四個人的小公司。根據新聞報導指出，台灣有八成的年輕人想自己創業，而且大部分都想從事餐飲業。

目前雖然經濟情況差，就業機會少，但是人生因為夢想而美麗，有夢最美。有句話說：「機會是留給準備好的人」，夢想的實現要經過許多努力及挑戰。培養實力，等待機會，相信每一個人都能擁有自己的片天。

### Text in Simplified Characters

别放弃梦想

在台湾生活了一段时间以后，马安同觉得台湾的物价比刚来的时候上涨了不少。有越来越多的台湾人抱怨这种低所得、高物价的情况，什么都涨了，就是薪水没涨。一般大学刚毕业的年人，不是找不到工作，就是每个月的薪水只能勉强生活。不少人辛苦工作了很多年，却根本存不了什么钱，更别说有能力买房子了。有些人还成立了「无壳蜗牛」组织，抗议台湾地价和房价高涨，造成贫富不均，人民买不起房子。

根据台湾政府的统计，每年的失业率大概在百分之三到五左右。虽然跟其他国家比起来，台湾的失业率并不特别高，但人民却觉得生活苦哈哈的，很多人都是「穷忙族」。这种现象一方面受到全球经济不景气的影响，另一方面是因为政府所施行的经济政策也有问题。大多数人的薪资多年未调高不说，还有不少人被裁员。在台湾找不到工作的，不得不中國大陸或别的国家另谋发展。

马安同今年在台湾过年时，他观察到一些有趣的现象。不少台湾人恨不得能一夜致富，大家喜欢买彩券，希望能中奖，常做发财梦。另外还有一个现象就是每个人都想当老板，不想看别人的脸色，所以在台湾有不少只有三、四个人的小公司。根据新聞報導指出，台湾有八成的年轻人想自己创业，而且大部分都想从事餐饮业。

目前虽然经济情况差，就业机会少，但是人生因为梦想而美丽，有梦最美。有句话说：「机会是留给准备好的人」，梦想的实现要经过许多努力及挑战。培养实力，等待机会，相信每一个人都能拥有自己的一片天。

### English

**Don't Give Up on Your Dreams**

After living in Taiwan for a while, Ma Antong feels prices have risen a lot since he first arrived. More and more Taiwanese complain about low income and high prices — everything goes up except salaries. Fresh graduates either can't find jobs or their monthly pay barely covers living expenses. Many work hard for years but can't save anything, let alone afford a house. Some have even founded the "Snails Without Shells" organization to protest rising land and housing prices that widen the rich-poor gap.

According to government statistics, Taiwan's unemployment rate is roughly 3-5%. Compared to other countries it's not particularly high, but people feel life is hard. Many are "busy poor" — working tirelessly yet achieving nothing. This stems partly from the global economic downturn and partly from flawed government policy. Salaries haven't risen in years, and layoffs are common. Those who can't find work in Taiwan have to go mainland China or elsewhere to seek opportunities.

During Chinese New Year in Taiwan, Ma Antong observed interesting phenomena. Many Taiwanese desperately want to get rich overnight, buying lottery tickets hoping to strike it rich. Everyone wants to be their own boss rather than work for someone else, so Taiwan has many tiny companies of three or four people. News reports say 80% of young Taiwanese want to start businesses, mostly in the food and beverage industry.

Yet even though the economy is sluggish and jobs are scarce, life is beautiful because of dreams — having dreams is the best. As the saying goes: "Opportunity favors the prepared mind." Achieving dreams takes effort and challenge. Cultivate your strength, wait for opportunity, and believe everyone can have their own piece of sky.

## 文法 Grammar

### I. 哪裡 rhetorical question — 05-05

**Function:** 哪裡 can also express a rhetorical question refuting or doubting, i.e., "how can it be possible?" or "cannot possibly..."

- 一般大學剛畢業的年輕人哪裡有錢買房子？
- 這件事哪裡稀奇了？這種現象越來越普遍了。
- 我哪裡是盲目升學？我以後還要念博士呢。
- 妳穿起來哪裡不好看？妳穿起來跟模特兒一樣漂亮。
- 我媽媽連電腦都不會用，哪裡會上網購物？

**Usage:** 哪裡, though originally an interrogative pronoun, refers to the meaning of "cannot possibly" here. This 哪裡 should be distinguished from the common response to "thank you" — 「哪裡！哪裡！」

### II. 畢竟 after all — 05-06

**Function:** With this adverb, the speaker urges the addressee to accept what cannot be helped or avoided.

- 雖然現在很多年輕人批評他，但我認為我們都應該尊敬他，畢竟他對國家還是有不少貢獻。
- 我們餐飲業還算景氣，不過畢竟物價上漲了不少，所以我的生活還是苦哈哈的。
- 聽古典音樂的人畢竟比較少，那場音樂會的票應該還買得到吧。
- 由於養狗畢竟比養孩子方便，因此很多人不養孩子而養狗。
- 整型畢竟還是有風險的，你再多考慮考慮吧。

**Usage:** As a member of the so-called "movable adverbs," 畢竟 can occur before a VP or at the beginning of a clause. Other such adverbs include 難道，大概，居然，隨時，到底 etc.

#### 練習 Exercise

3. 撞衫對她來說畢竟，所以她馬上就把外套脫掉。
4. 別擔心，他會另謀發展的，畢竟。
5. 找工作時外表畢承，所以有越來越多人能接受微整型手術了。

### III. 的確 indeed — 05-07

**Function:** This adverb re-affirms a view previously presented.

- A：我聽說現在有不少大學生想延後畢業的時間，是真的嗎？
  B: 現在的確有不少學生，因為要逃避就業而延後畢業的時間。
- A：我覺得上網交好心容易喔！
  B: 上網交友的確容易，不過要小心喔！
- A：上次你說你以後要自己創業，為什麼呢？
  B: 我畢業以後，的確想自己創業，因為不想看別人的臉色。
- A：現在的房價高漲，大多數人都買不起房子吧？
  B: 的確，我跟很多人一樣買不起房子，我們都是「無殼蝸牛」。
- A：最近我的眼睛非常疲倦，可能是因為工作時一直盯著螢幕看吧！
  B: 長時間一直盯著螢幕看，眼睛的確會很疲倦。

**Usage:**

1. 的確 can be used at the beginning of a sentence, meaning "I agree with you," e.g., 的確，現在即使有碩士學位的人找工作也不容易。
2. 的確 can be reduplicated: 的的確確, e.g., 他的的確確是昨天才知道的。

The reduplicated form of 的確 is quite colloquial and puts added stress on whatever follows it, making the tone stronger. It is worth noting that only very few disyllabic adverbs can be reduplicated, as below:

- 完全一完完全全 (B2, L12)，幾乎一幾幾乎 (B3, L03)
- 隨便一隨隨便便 (B3, L06)，確實一確確實實 (B4, L03)

### IV. 話說回來 however, on the other hand — 05-08

**Function:** With this phrase, the speaker first affirms the merit of a previous statement and then presents a qualification from a different perspective.

- 雖然帶現金比較麻煩。可是話說回來，刷卡風險比較大，信用卡容易被盜用。
- 電信公司的合約已經寫得很清楚了。不過話說回來，他是外國人，應該要再解釋一下才行。
- 用手機祝朋友生日快樂的確很方便。但話說回來，好像少了一點感覺。
- 對她來說，包餃子不難。不過話說回來，要包一百多個餃子也沒那麼容易。
- 你應該趁在台灣的時候多出去逛逛。但是話說回來，學校的功課也很重要。

**Usage:** This phrase is often preceded by such conjunctions as 可是，但是, and 不過, e.g., 住在捷運站附近的確很方便。不過話說回來，那裡的房價都很貴。

5. 學校的校刊的確不應該只採訪校友，可是話說回來，。

### V. NP 之所以 S2 是因為 S1 — 05-09

**Function:** This pattern inverts S1 (cause) and S2 (effect) and places special emphasis on the new information (S1). NP is the subject of both S1 and S2.

- 他之所以熬夜是因為要準備明天的考試。
- 我之所以要學西班牙文，是因為明年想去西班牙旅行。
- 我的房間之所以這麼亂，是因為我是上個禮拜才剛搬來的。
- 「靠爸族」及「啃老族」之所以越來越多，是因為年輕人不願意自食其力。
- 我之所以買小農種的蔬菜，是因為他們不用農藥。

**Usage:** This is a highly formal pattern and should be reserved for formal occasions, not for ordinary conversations.

### VI. 根本 not...at all — 05-10

**Function:** With the adverb 根本, the speaker emphatically presents a negative view.

- 他每天在夜市裡擺地攤，收攤的時間都很晚，根本沒有時間去約會。
- 你根本就看不懂現代舞，何必要買那麼貴的票？
- 台灣的房價那麼高，一般人根本買不起房子。
- 現在是宅經濟時代，根本不必去百貨公司買衣服，線上購物就好了。
- 他們根本沒有什麼實力，只是運氣好而已。

### VII. 更別說…了 let alone, never mind — 05-11

**Function:** This pattern presents a new topic, to which the previous statement applies even more effectively.

- 一到週末百貨公司裡人就很多，更別說百貨公司週年慶的時候了。
- 你這麼有學問的人都不懂，更別說我了。
- 他說的地方連我這個當地人都找不到，更別說你們了。
- 我來台灣以後每天忙著念書，連台北101都還沒去過，更別說平溪老街了。
- 我連小獎都沒中過，更別說中大獎了。

**Usage:**

1. 更別說 is followed by either an NP or a VP.
2. This pattern is often used together with 連…都… and 就是…也… patterns, e.g.,
   - 連你都不清楚，更別說我這個新來的人了。
   - 就是我也可能遲到，更別說他了。

### VIII. 恨不得 one really wishes one could... — 05-12

**Function:** The phrase 恨不得 expresses a strong desire to do something against all odds.

- 住在美國時，我真恨不得每天早上都能吃到燒餅油條或中式飯糰。
- 他好不容易考上了熱門科系，念了一個學期卻發現興趣不合，念得很痛苦，恨不得能馬上轉系。
- 水餃被我煮破了不少，不夠的話怎麼辦？真恨不得我們還有時間能再包一些。可是我們得出門了。
- 要不是我媽媽反對，我也恨不得能跟你們一起去參加反核遊行。
- 走路走得腿痛死了，真恨不得能搭計程車過去，可惜錢包裡只有50塊錢。

**Usage:**

1. Most often used with auxiliary verbs like wish, want, would. Cannot be used in the negative: e.g., *我恨不得不可以馬上回家。
2. All pronouns can serve as the subject though 我 is the commonest.

## 語法例句 Grammar Examples in English

### I. 哪裡 rhetorical question

- How could young university graduates possibly afford to buy a house?
- What's so strange about it? It's become rather common actually.
- How could she possibly shop online? She doesn't even know how to use a computer.
- What do you mean I keep studying just for the sake of studying? I have plans to do a PhD.
- What do you mean it doesn't look good on you? You look like a model in that.

### II. 畢竟 after all

- He has come under a lot of criticism among younger people, but I think we still ought to respect him. After all he has done so much for our country.
- Our food and beverage industry is doing well, but after all, commodity prices have risen quite a bit, so times are still hard for me.
- There aren't many people that listen to classical music after all, so there should still be tickets available for the performance.
- After all it's much easier to have a dog than a child, so that's what a lot of people are doing.
- Cosmetic surgery does have its risks after all. Make sure you give it due thought.

### III. 的確 indeed

- A: I've heard that a lot of college students are putting off graduation. Is that true?
  B: Indeed, many students postpone graduation to avoid having to get a job.
- A: Making friends online is real easy!
  B: It is indeed, but be discerning!
- A: I remember you talked about starting your own business. Why is that?
  B: Indeed, I want to start my own business after graduation because I don't want to have to deal with bosses.
- A: Housing prices have risen sharply. Most people can't afford to buy a house, right?
  B: You're absolutely right. Like a lot of other people, I too can't afford one. We're all like a snail without a shell.
- A: My eyes have been extremely sore lately. I think it's because I have to stare at a screen all day for work.
  B: Looking at a screen for extended periods of time will certainly do that to you.

### IV. 話說回來 however, on the other hand

- It's a hassle always carrying cash, but on the other hand there is a greater risk with paying by plastic.
- That telecom company's contract is very clear. However, on the other hand he's a foreigner, so further explanation might be needed.
- Sending a birthday wish to a friend via cell phone is easy, but on the other hand it feels a little empty.
- Making dumplings is not hard for her, but on the other hand making 100 of them is no easy task.
- You should take the opportunity to experience Taiwan while you are here, but on the other hand, schoolwork is important too.

### V. NP之所以S2是因為S1

- His whole purpose for staying up late is to prepare for tomorrow's test.
- I want to study Spanish because next year I will be travelling to Spain.
- My room is such a mess because I only moved in last week.
- Sponges and parasites are increasing in number because the younger generation doesn't want to earn their own living.
- I buy produce from small-scale farmers because it is chemical free.

### VI. 根本 not...at all

- He sets up a stand at the night market every day, and by the time he packs up it is so late that there is no way he can make the meet-up afterwards.
- You don't understand the first thing about modern dance, so why go and buy such an expensive ticket?
- Housing prices in Taiwan are exorbitant. There is no way the average joe can afford it.
- It's the on-line economy generation. There's no longer any need to go to department stores for clothes. Everything can be ordered online.
- They have no actual prowess at all, just good luck.

### VII. 更別說…了 let alone, never mind

- The department stores are packed even on the weekends, not to mention during anniversary sales.
- If someone as highly educated as you doesn't understand it, what chance do I have.
- I grew up here and even I can't find it. How can you possibly find it?
- After I came to Taiwan I was so busy studying that I didn't have a chance to see Taipei 101, let alone the old street in Pingxi.
- I've never even won a small prize, let alone a big one.

### VIII. 恨不得 one really wishes one could... — 05-12

- When I was living in the US, every morning I wanted so badly to have those deep fried breadsticks wrapped in a roll and Chinese style onigri.
- He worked so hard to get into a good department in university, but after struggling for one term found out it wasn't for him, and wanted nothing more than to change majors.
- I over-boiled quite some of the dumplings, some split open. What if there isn't enough? I really wish we had the time to make some more, but we have to get going.
- Too bad my mom is against it. I really wish I could join up for the rally against nuclear energy.
- My legs are so sore from walking and I want nothing more than to take a taxi. Too bad I only have 50 dollars in my wallet.

## 中華文化點滴 Bits of Chinese Culture

### Office Antics: Group Buying Food

There is an interesting phenomenon taking place in offices around Taiwan: collectively purchasing food. Comparatively speaking, this type of co-worker group purchase is a rarity in other countries, where most people shop online only after work, and consumer behaviors are much more personalized. According to an analysis from one online platform, online shoppers of food products in Taiwan are mostly female "young professionals" aged 21-30, and "office working wives" aged 31-40.

Group buying saves consumers the trouble of having to go out and run around shopping. It is also a way for them to get goods at a low price, distract themselves from the banality of office work, and stay connected with friends. The build-up of putting in an order, the consumption of goods, and ensuing discussion all excite gabble that lightens up the office atmosphere. The thrill that surrounds an order delivery is almost like that of winning a lottery.

Office workers work hard every day, so when a group of them get together to make a collective food purchase, whether as a solution for stress or the munchies, nobody can stop them. Too far? We deliver! Too expensive? We'll give a discount! It takes just a few clicks of the mouse for food to show up at the door, so you can sit back and enjoy some tasty treats right in the office!

### Beauty Queen Marketing

In Taiwan, there is something special about trade shows and sales in general: As long as gorgeous women endorse a product, sales do particularly well. For computer, information technology, and automotive exhibitions, manufacturers send out their most ostentatious 'showgirls' as a way of attracting potential buyers, and it goes without saying that otaku — obsessive anime geeks/gamers — show up in hordes at video game conventions. As the cosplay girls — each more beautiful than the last — are repeatedly lit up by camera flashes, gawking otaku can't help but take out their cash and credit cards to buy video game points. Rather surprisingly, events such as this have lead to the creation of an otaku-fueled economic miracle.

It could be said that 檳榔西施 (Betel Nut Beauties) are the pioneers of beauty queen marketing; they are the young girls wearing scant and sexy clothing selling betel nut on the side of the road. These days, if an exhibition on bedding, building material, wedding photography, cuisine, or even agricultural products is to attract crowds and feed buyer's hunger, having a beauty queen representing, hosting, or selling the products is standard practice. Doesn't a bowl of noodles served by a beauty queen just smell better? Don't the drinks they serve go down smoother? Stores near university campuses also compete for the looks of customers by employing beauties, and judging by their names — Fruit Beauties, Chicken Steak Beauties, Black Tea Beauties — not only are the oval faces of these saleswomen very pretty, they put up pretty numbers too.
