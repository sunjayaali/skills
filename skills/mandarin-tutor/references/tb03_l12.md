# 第十二課 政治

## 學習目標 Learning Objectives

**Topic: 政治 Politics**

- Learn to discuss issues related to elections for elected representatives.
- Learn to analyze logic and offer various possibilities.
- Learn to express your views and reasons for opposing something.
- Learn to introduce candidates and their experience in politics.

## 對話 Dialogue

*(12-01)*

**安德思：** 何雅婷，妳現在有空嗎？能不能幫我看一下這份作業？

*Ān Dé sī: Hé Yǎtíng, nǐ xiànzài yǒu kòng ma? Néngbu néng bāng wǒ kàn yīxià zhè fèn zuòyè?*

**何雅婷：** 什麼作業？紅毛城的故事？你現在學台灣歷史嗎？

*Hé Yǎtíng: Shénme zuòyè? Hóngmáochéng de gùshì? Nǐ xiànzài xué Táiwān lìshǐ ma?*

**安德思：** 是啊。下個星期一輪到我報告紅毛城的歷史。可是我的中文不太好，有些資料看不懂，想請妳幫忙。

*Ān Dé sī: Shì a. Xià ge xīngqī yī lún dào wǒ bàogào hóngmáochéng de lìshǐ. Kěshì wǒ de Zhōngwén bú tài hǎo, yǒuxiē zīliào kàn bù dǒng, xiǎng qǐng nǐ bāngmáng.*

**何雅婷：** 沒問題。網路上怎麼說的？

*Hé Yǎtíng: Méi wèntí. Wǎnglù shàng zěnme shuō de?*

**安德思：** 網路上說，荷蘭人在1624年来到台湾，在淡水建了红毛城。后来西班牙人到了台湾北部，也在淡水建了城。1642年，荷兰派军队打败了西班牙人。西班牙人离开台湾以前，把城烧了。所以现在的红毛城是荷兰人重建的。

*Ān Dé sī: Wǎnglù shàng shuō, Hélán rén zài 1624 nián lái dào Táiwān, zài Dànshuǐ jiàn le hóngmáochéng. Hòulái Xībānyà rén dào le Táiwān běibù, yě zài Dànshuǐ jiàn le chéng. 1642 nián, Hélán pài jūnduì dǎ bài le Xībānyà rén. Xībānyà rén líkāi Táiwān yǐqián, bǎ chéng shāo le. Suǒyǐ xiànzài de hóngmáochéng shì Hélán rén chóngjiàn de.*

**安德思：** 紅毛城為什麼叫紅毛城？因為城牆是紅色的嗎？

*Ān Dé sī: Hóngmáochéng wèishéme jiào hóngmáochéng? Yīnwèi chéngqiáng shì hóngsè de ma?*

**何雅婷：** 不是。不是。是因為西方人頭髮的顏色關係。那時候的台灣人叫西方人「紅毛」。

*Hé Yǎtíng: Bú shì. Bú shì. Shì yīnwèi xīfāng rén tóufa de yánsè guānxì. Nàshíhou de Táiwān rén jiào xīfāng rén 「hóngmáo」.*

**安德思：** 那時候台灣沒有政府嗎？為什麼歐洲人可以自由地來來去去，甚至在大陸打仗？

*Ān Dé sī: Nàshíhou Táiwān méiyǒu zhèngfǔ ma? Wèishéme Ōuzhōu rén kěyǐ zìyóu de láilái qùqù, shènzhì zài Dàlù dǎzhàn?*

**何雅婷：** 那時候台灣不是一個國家，沒有政府。除了少數來開墾跟做生意的漢人以外，都是原住民。

*Hé Yǎtíng: Nàshíhou Táiwān búshì yīge guójiā, méiyǒu zhèngfǔ. Chúle shǎoshù lái kāikěn gēn zuò shēngyì de hànrén yǐwai, dōu shì yuánzhùmín.*

**安德思：** 這麼說，台灣應該有很多原住民才對。可是為什麼我的朋友當中一個原住民都沒有？

*Ān Dé sī: Zhème shuō, Táiwān yīnggāi yǒu hěnduō yuánzhùmín cái duì. Kěshì wèishéme wǒ de péngyou dāngzhōng yīge yuánzhùmín dōu méiyǒu?*

**何雅婷：** 因為原住民都住山里，離城市太遠了。你的朋友是誰？

*Hé Yǎtíng: Yīnwèi yuánzhùmín dōu zhù shānlǐ, lí chéngshì tài yuǎn le. Nǐ de péngyou shì shuí?*

**安德思：** 高橋健太。他們說他是「外來者」。

*Ān Dé sī: Gāo Qiáo Jiàntài. Tāmen shuō tā shì «wàiláizhě».*

**何雅婷：** 誰說他是外來者？他是日本人嗎？

*Hé Yǎtíng: Shuí shuō tā shì wàiláizhě? Tā shì Rìběnrén ma?*

**安德思：** 我們的政治老師說，荷蘭人來台灣以前，台灣沒有政府，沒有國家。高橋健太的爺爺是日本人，他們一家從日本來台灣，已經四代了。

*Ān Dé sī: Wǒmen de zhèngzhì lǎoshī shuō, Hélán rén lái Táiwān yǐqián, Táiwān méiyǒu zhèngfǔ, méiyǒu guójiā. Gāo Qiáo Jiàntài de yéye shì Rìběnrén, tāmen yījiā cóng Rìběn lái Táiwān, yǐjīng sì dài le.*

**何雅婷：** 那他應該是台灣人呀。

*Hé Yǎtíng: Nà tā yīnggāi shì Táiwān rén ya.*

**安德思：** 我們的政治老師說，日本人從1895年開始統治台灣，到1945年結束。所以高橋健太的爺爺是日本人，他們一家從日本來台灣，已經四代了。

*Ān Dé sī: Wǒmen de zhèngzhì lǎoshī shuō, Rìběnrén cóng 1895 nián kāishǐ tǒngzhì Táiwān, dào 1945 nián jiéshù. Suǒyǐ Gāo Qiáo Jiàntài de yéye shì Rìběnrén, tāmen yījiā cóng Rìběn lái Táiwān, yǐjīng sì dài le.*

**何雅婷：** 喔，那他的家人都已經台灣化了。

*Hé Yǎtíng: Wō, nà tā de jiārén dōu yǐjīng Táiwān huà le.*

**安德思：** 可是高橋健太說，他覺得自己是日本人，不過他的女朋友是台灣人。

*Ān Dé sī: Kěshì Gāo Qiáo Jiàntài shuō, tā juéde zìjǐ shì Rìběnrén, búguò tā de nǚpéngyou shì Táiwān rén.*

**何雅婷：** 那他為什麼不回去日本呢？

*Hé Yǎtíng: Nà tā wèishéme bù huíqù Rìběn ma?*

**安德思：** 他說他覺得台灣比較好玩，而且他的女朋友在台灣。

*Ān Dé sī: Tā shuō tā juéde Táiwān bǐjiào hǎo wán, érqiě tā de nǚpéngyou zài Táiwān.*

**何雅婷：** 哦，那也對。

*Hé Yǎtíng: Ó, nà yě duì.*

*(12-02)*

**何雅婷：** 安德思，你對政治有興趣嗎？

*Hé Yǎtíng: Ān Dé sī, nǐ duì zhèngzhì yǒu xìngqù ma?*

**安德思：** 有啊。我今天去投票了。

*Ān Dé sī: Yǒu a. Wǒ jīntiān qù tóupiào le.*

**何雅婷：** 真的吗？你投票給誰？

*Hé Yǎtíng: Zhēn de ma? Nǐ tóupiào gěi shuí?*

**安德思：** 我投票給陳敏萱。

*Ān Dé sī: Wǒ tóupiào gěi Chén Mǐnxuān.*

**何雅婷：** 陳敏萱？她不是去參加遊行了嗎？

*Hé Yǎtíng: Chén Mǐnxuān? Tā búshì qù cānbān yóuxíng le ma?*

**安德思：** 是的，她今天早上參加了一個反核遊行。下午才來投票。

*Ān Dé sī: Shì de, tā jīntiān zǎoshang cānbān le yí ge fǎn hé yóuxíng. Xiàwǔ cái lái tóupiào.*

**何雅婷：** 那她候選……啊，她參選了？

*Hé Yǎtíng: Nà tā hòuxuǎn …… a, tā cān xuǎn le?*

**安德思：** 嗯，她登記了。不過她的對手是個資深政治人物，她的支援者很多。我擔心她不會當選。

*Ān Dé sī: Òm, tā dēngjì le. Búguò tā de duìshǒu shì ge zīshēn zhèngzhì rénwù, tā de zhīyuán zhě hěn duō. Wǒ dānxīn tā bú huì dāngxuǎn.*

**何雅婷：** 那你覺得她會輸嗎？

*Hé Yǎtíng: Nà nǐ juéde tā huì shū ma?*

**安德思：** 我不知道。不过按照宪法，有投票权了。不过年底的选举我不会去投票的。

*Ān Dé sī: Wǒ bù zhīdào. Búguò ànzhào xiànfǎ, yǒu tóupiào quán le. Búguò niándǐ de xuǎnjǔ wǒ bú huì qù tóupiào de.*

**何雅婷：** 你今年滿二十歲了？

*Hé Yǎtíng: Nǐ jīnnián mǎn èrshí suì le?*

**安德思：** 是啊。我今年滿二十歲了。按照憲法的规定，有投票权了。

*Ān Dé sī: Shì a. Wǒ jīntiān mǎn èrshí suì le. Ànzhào xiànfǎ de guīdìng, yǒu tóupiào quán le.*

**何雅婷：** 那你去投票了吗？

*Hé Yǎtíng: Nà nǐ qù tóupiào le ma?*

**安德思：** 去了。我今天早上去投票了。不過我只不過開開玩笑而已，你怎麼就生氣了？

*Ān Dé sī: Qù le. Wǒ jīntiān zǎoshang qù tóupiào le. Búguò wǒ zhībùguò kāi kāi wánxiào ér yǐ, nǐ zěnme jiù shēngqì le?*

**何雅婷：** 我不是生氣，我是擔心。你這樣隨便說說而已，哪裡是真的想養狗？

*Hé Yǎtíng: Wǒ búshì shēngqì, wǒ shì dānxīn. Nǐ zhège yàng suíbiàn shuōshuō ér yǐ, nǎlǐ shì zhēn de xiǎng yǎng gǒu?*

**安德思：** 我才開開玩笑而已，你怎麼就生氣了？

*Ān Dé sī: Wǒ cái kāi kāi wánxiào ér yǐ, nǐ zěnme jiù shēngqì le?*

**何雅婷：** 你不該這樣說。

*Hé Yǎtíng: Nǐ bú gāi zhège yàng shuō.*

**陳敏萱：** 安德思，你為什麼氣餒？

### 為人民服務的路 The Road for Serving the People

**李文彥：** 叔叔，您退休後想幹什麼？

**叔叔：** 我啊，想從政。

**李文彥：** 從政？您一個大學教授，還去選民意代表？

**叔叔：** 為什麼不能？我認為只有好的政策才能為人民帶來幸福、安定的生活。為了理想，他辭掉了工作，參加民意代表選舉。因為頭腦清楚、口才好，再加上常上電視對許多政治改革的議題提出看法，因此很受選民的歡迎。尤其是他在教育改革跟國民年金方面的努力，讓他得到選民普遍的支持，順利地連任了四屆。

**李文彥：** 那後來呢？

**叔叔：** 不過，上一屆的選舉，雖然他的民意支持度在所有的候選人當中一直是最高的，結果卻落選了。他覺得很失誤。他本來以為，中間選民會把票投給他，沒想到得票率不如預期。太太看他這麼難過，建議暫時先回鄉下老家休息一陣子，再考慮下一步怎麼走。

**李文彥：** 那他现在怎麼樣？

Li Wenyan's uncle was a well known university professor with a high status in academic circles. But he had always wanted to go into politics. After he returned to his hometown, because his image in politics was good, the people of his hometown would seek "Uncle" out to discuss all kinds of matters, both large and small. Soon afterward, the owner of a publishing company was going to retire, but his children were not willing to take over his business. In addition, he even sold organic agricultural products for small farmers in his bookstore and used the profits as funds for the English classes.

Although his days were productive and Uncle was very satisfied with his current life, as the elections got closer and closer, he also began to think about whether or not to come out again and run for public representative. Perhaps, by returning to the world of politics, he would have power and would be able to serve more people. But perhaps, by leaving politics and leaving the city, he would more easily be able to realize his dream of serving others. Who knows?

## 文法 Grammar

**I-1 只不過(而已)** *(12-05)*

**Function:** This pattern consists of three elements: the adverb 只, the adverb 不過, and the sentence particle 而已. They are near synonyms, meaning "only, merely." Thus, this pattern is an instance of 3-way reinforcement and means "merely, just." 而已 can be omitted.

**Usage:**

1. **Omission in multiple reinforcement:** In this pattern, the omission goes like this: ending with 而已 alone, 不過「」而已, and finally 只不過「」而已. The first two options are illustrated below.

   a. 他隨便說說而已。哪裡是真的想養狗？
   b. 一個晚上沒睡好而已，就沒有精神上課了。

2. **不過「」而已**
   a. 不過18度而已，你怎麼就穿起羊毛外套來了？
   b. 三號候選人不過是形象好而已，從政經驗其實並不多。

3. **只不過 vs. 才** (Vol. 2, Lesson 3, Grammar point 9):

   (1) When a sentence contains a number in the object position, either 只不過 or 才 can be used.
      e.g., a. 雖然是百貨公司週年慶，可是商品只不過打八折而已。
            b. 雖然是百貨公司週年慶，可是商品才打八折而已。
      There is, however, a difference in attitude on the part of the speaker. Although 只不過 also stresses that the amount mentioned is small, it is more factual while 才 further indicates that the quantity falls short of expectation.

   (2) Therefore, when you want to stress something has fallen short of common expectation, you can only really use 才.
      e.g., a. "你擺了一個時上的攤子，怎麼只不過賺了幾百塊錢？"
            b. "你擺了一個平上的攤子，怎麼才賺了幾百塊錢？"

   (3) However, when 只不過 is followed by something other than an amount, 只不過 cannot be substituted with 才.

**I-5 為** *(12-09)*

**Function:** The literary preposition 為 introduces a beneficiary noun into the sentence, a noun which benefits from the context of the following verb phrase. The term beneficiary can have many meanings.

1. Recipient of the action. In this usage, the object of 為 seems to be the real object of the verb. E.g., (1) 為人民服務 (Serve on behalf of the people) (2) 為大家加油 (Root for everybody) (3) 為農夫帶來許多好處 (Bring many advantages to farmers).
2. On behalf of. In this usage, the verbs are mostly state verbs. E.g., (1) 為孩子擔心 (worry for/about the children) (2) 為家人難過 (feel bad for my family) (3) 為朋友高興 (happy for my friend).
3. For the sake of. In this usage, something has been done for the benefit of someone. E.g., (1) 為他們買健康保險 (buy health insurance for their sake) (2) 為他翻譯 (translate for his sake) (3) 為誰辛苦 (work hard for whose sake?).

## 中華文化點滴 Bits of Chinese Culture

**Elections and Numbers**

In Chinese culture, homophones (characters with similar sounds) play a very important role. Employed well, homophones can bring good luck. Their use is not only a must for everything from weddings and other joyous occasions to funerals; they are also used in elections, where in white-hot contests, candidates fight over every vote.

Despite the fact that some people scorn the use of homophones and say that it's all just a bunch of hooey, candidates continue to use them on the off chance that the one they use might bring them luck and have some effect on the outcome of the election.

In Taiwan, in the heat of campaigns, characterized by tricks galore as well as slogans and placards designed to leave a deep impression on the hearts and minds of the electorate, thinking of some way to make voters remember your number on the ballot is also an essential part of running for office. As a consequence, the day that candidates draw lots to see who gets what number is a big deal, and how they can use homophones of the numbers to their advantage is a good way to manufacture newsworthy discussion.

For example, a candidate who draws the number 3 might make an "OK" sign with his thumb and index finger while the other three fingers are fanned out to the side to suggest that "If I'm elected, everything will be OK." Regardless of what number a candidate draws, he has to be able to immediately make some similar lucky gesture or shout out some auspicious homophone. It's just part of the game of politics in Taiwan.

- Candidates who draw the number 1 use a thumbs up sign.
- Candidates who draw the number 2 use the Victory Sign.
- Candidates who draw the number 3 use the OK Sign.
- Candidates who draw the number 6, which represents 福祿 (flu), will shout 六六大順 (liu liu da shun, everything goes smoothly) to indicate that he is confident that everything will go smoothly.
- The one who draws 7 will shout 開得勝 (kai desheng, open the flag and win victory) to give himself momentum.
- Even the candidate that draws 4, the least favorite number, says, 事事如意、勢在必得 (shishi ruyi, shizai bide) — bide, everything will go smoothly, the election is in the bag — to turn the tables in his favor.
