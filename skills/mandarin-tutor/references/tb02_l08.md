Learning Kungfu

## 學習目標 Learning Objectives

**Topic: 休閒活動 Leisure Activities**

- Learning how to talk about my hobbies.

- Learning how to accept or decline invitations.

## 對話 Dialogue

**東健：** 安同！安同！

*Dōngjiàn: Āntóng! Āntóng!*

Dongjian: Antong! Antong!

**安同：** 東健！對不起，沒聽見你叫我。

*Āntóng: Dōngjiàn! Duìbùqǐ, méi tīngjiàn nǐ jiào wǒ.*

Antong: Dongjian! I'm sorry. I didn't hear you calling.

**東健：** 音樂那麼吵，你當然聽不見。

*Dōngjiàn: Yīnyuè nàme chǎo, nǐ dāngrán tīng bù jiàn.*

Dongjian: The music is so loud. Of course you can't hear anything.

**安同：** 有什麼事嗎？

*Āntóng: Yǒu shénme shì ma?*

Antong: What's up?

**東健：** 沒事，只是一到學校門口，就看見你推著腳踏車走過來。

*Dōngjiàn: Méi shì, zhǐshì yí dào xuéxiào ménkǒu, jiù kànjiàn nǐ tuī zhe jiǎotàchē zǒu guòlái.*

Dongjian: Nothing in particular. When I got to the school gate, I just happened to see you walking your bike this way.

**安同：** 你下午要做什麼？

*Āntóng: Nǐ xiàwǔ yào zuò shénme?*

Antong: What are you doing this afternoon?

**東健：** 我要去體育館運動。對了，你現在還在學太極拳嗎？

*Dōngjiàn: Wǒ yào qù tǐyùguǎn yùndòng. Duì le, nǐ xiànzài hái zài xué tàijíquán ma?*

Dongjian: I am going to the gym to workout. By the way, are you still studying Tai Chi?

**安同：** 是啊！我的師父說我有天分，學得不錯。

*Āntóng: Shì a! Wǒ de shīfù shuō wǒ yǒu tiānfèn, xué de búcuò.*

Antong: Yes. My shifu (i.e., master, teacher) said I have talent and am learning nicely.

**東健：** 你師父是台灣人吧？他說中文你聽得懂嗎？

*Dōngjiàn: Nǐ shīfù shì Táiwānrén ba? Tā shuō Zhōngwén nǐ tīng de dǒng ma?*

Dongjian: I gather your shifu is Taiwanese. Can you understand his Chinese?

**安同：** 剛開始我都聽不懂，可是現在沒問題了。

*Āntóng: Gāng kāishǐ wǒ dōu tīng bù dǒng, kěshì xiànzài méi wèntí le.*

Antong: At first, I couldn't understand anything, but now it's not a problem.

**東健：** 太極拳難嗎？你學了多久了？

*Dōngjiàn: Tàijíquán nán ma? Nǐ xué le duōjiǔ le?*

Dongjian: Is Tai Chi hard? How long have you been studying it?

**安同：** 不太容易，而且要很早起來。我學了三個多月了，才學會了一點。

*Āntóng: Bú tài róngyì, érqiě yào hěn zǎo qǐlái. Wǒ xué le sān gè duō yuè le, cái xuéhuì le yìdiǎn.*

Antong: It's not all that easy, and I also need to wake up early to practice. I have been studying for three months and have only learned a little.

**東健：** 可是太極拳的動作看起來慢慢的，輕輕的，應該很輕鬆。

*Dōngjiàn: Kěshì tàijíquán de dòngzuò kàn qǐlái mànman de, qīngqīng de, yīnggāi hěn qīngsōng.*

Dongjian: But movements in Tai Chi look slow and gentle. It should be easy to learn.

**安同：** 那些動作好像很容易，但是剛開始的時候，我早上常常累得起不來，腿也痛得走不了路。

*Āntóng: Nàxiē dòngzuò hǎoxiàng hěn róngyì, dànshì gāng kāishǐ de shíhou, wǒ zǎoshang chángcháng lèi de qǐ bù lái, tuǐ yě tòng de zǒu bù liǎo lù.*

Antong: Those moves look easy, but when I first started I was so exhausted, I couldn't even get up in the morning and my legs hurt so much, I couldn't walk.

**東健：** 那你怎麼還學得下去？

*Dōngjiàn: Nà nǐ zěnme hái xué de xiàqù?*

Dongjian: Then how were you able to keep studying?

**安同：** 雖然很累，可是這是我的興趣。

*Āntóng: Suīrán hěn lèi, kěshì zhè shì wǒ de xìngqù.*

Antong: It's tiring, but this is where my interest lies.

**東健：** 你下午沒事吧？要不要跟我一起去運動？

*Dōngjiàn: Nǐ xiàwǔ méi shì ba? Yào bú yào gēn wǒ yìqǐ qù yùndòng?*

Dongjian: I gather you have nothing to do this afternoon. Do you want to go with me to work out?

**安同：** 好啊！我先去圖書館借書，下午再跟你去運動。

*Āntóng: Hǎo a! Wǒ xiān qù túshūguǎn jiè shū, xiàwǔ zài gēn nǐ qù yùndòng.*

Antong: Sure. I'll go to the library first to borrow a book and go with you to exercise in the afternoon.

## 閱讀 Reading

今天李東健跟馬安同在體育館運動的時候，安同一直跟他說學太極拳的好處，還問他有沒有興趣，下次練習的時候可以帶他一起去。

東健想起安同剛開始學功夫的時候，常跟他抱怨，師父每次練習以前，都要大家先跑一千公尺，安同總是最後一個跑完，有的時候還跑不完。現在，東健發現安同的體力比三個月以前好多了，上了整天的課，安同還是很有精神。

李東健本來以為學功夫要花很多時間，安同可能沒有時間念書了，但是安同為了多練習，每天一下課，就回宿舍念書、做功課。做完了功課，再去練習。雖然練習的時間不少，但是一點都沒影響安同的成績。

李東健看馬安同學了太極拳以後，身體健康了，功課也進步了，覺得練太極拳的好處真不少，決定明天就跟安同說他也要學太極拳。

## 文法 Grammar

### I. 見 jiàn as a Perceptual Result

**Function:** When 見 follows a perceptual verb, it indicates that the object of an action (such as of seeing or hearing) is perceived.

- 昨天我在夜市看見小美了。
- 你說的，我都聽見了。
- 你為什麼在找書？我們都看見你把書拿回去了。

**Structures:**

**Negation:**

- 東西在哪裡？我沒看見啊！
- 我沒聽見他說什麼。
- 請你再說一次，我沒聽見。

**Questions:**

- 我的背包，你看見了沒有？
- 老師說的，你是不是聽見了？
- 張先生呢？你看見他走出來了嗎？

**Usage:** There's a clear difference in meaning between using a perception verb alone or with the result 見.

- 我沒聽他說話。
- 我沒聽見他說話。

### II. 懂 dǒng as a Cognitive Result

**Function:** When a verb is followed by 懂 as a result complement, the combination indicates that 'understanding' has taken place, i.e., 'understood' through listening (聽懂) or through reading (看懂).

- 老師說的，我都聽懂了。
- 老師給的功課我只聽了一次，就聽懂了。
- 老師寫的那些字，學生都看懂了。

**Structures:**

**Negation:** Negation is always 沒.

- 他說什麼？我沒聽懂。
- 那個電影我第一次沒看懂，第二次看懂了。
- 老師說明了半天，可是我沒聽懂。

**Questions:**

- 他剛說的，你是不是聽懂了？
- 這些資料你都看懂了沒有？
- 老師教的，你聽懂沒聽懂？

**Usage:**

1. "Complement" is a cover term in Chinese grammar for a variety of functions.
2. 懂 is a process verb and can be used alone as a main verb. It often serves as a complement to such verbs as 看 and 聽 to express the result of "understood". Compare the following sentences:

   (1) 老師教的，你懂了嗎？
   (2) 老師教的，你聽懂了嗎？
   (3) 老師教的，你看懂了嗎？

### III. 會 huì as an Achievement Result

**Function:** 會 is an auxiliary verb but can also serve as a complement of such verbs as 學，教，練 to express the result of 'mastery' and 'achievement'.

- 我在大學的時候學會怎麼開車。
- 只有一個鐘頭的課，老師要教會學生怎麼寫書法很難。
- 學生學會了用中文介紹自己。

**Structures:**

**Negation:**

- 我學游泳只學了一個禮拜，還沒學會。
- 師父教了兩個星期了，還沒教會馬安同太極拳最難的動作。
- 我們都沒學會怎麼騎腳踏車。

**Questions:**

- 這些字你是不是都學會了？
- 太極拳學了這麼久，你還沒學會啊？
- 馬安同教會那些小孩踢足球了沒有？

**Usage:** The auxiliary verb 會 can also be used as a state verb in very limited circumstances, such as in 我會西班牙語 'I know (how to speak) Spanish'. In combination, 學會 indicates that the subject acquires some ability through learning, while 教會 indicates that the learner is given instruction and thus acquires some ability.

### IV. Potential Capability with -得 de- or -不 bu-

**Function:** When 得 or 不 is inserted between the verb and the resultative or directional complement, the pattern expresses whether the result is potentially attainable.

- 我看得懂中國電影。
- 垃圾車的音樂雖然很小，但是我聽得見。
- 早上八點，我當然起得來。
- 師父教的這個動作很容易，每個人都學得會。

**Structures:**

**Negation:** Verb + 不 + complement

- 我認識他，但是他的名字我想不起來。
- 夜市太吵了，他聽不見我叫他。
- 半夜三點太早了，誰也起不來。

**Questions:**

- 老師上課，你聽得懂聽不懂？
- 廣告上的字那麼小，你是不是看不見？
- 只有一個星期，他們學得會學不會一百個漢字？

**Usage:** There is a difference between "沒 V₁V₂" and "V₁ 不 V₂". The first pattern indicates that some result was not accomplished. The second pattern indicates that some result cannot be accomplished. Compare the following examples:

- 我沒看見那個人從七樓走上來。
- 我在十樓，所以看不見七樓的人。

### V. Capability Complement 了 liǎo

**Function:** 了 liǎo is a capability complement, used only in the potential construction. 了 means that a result can be achieved.

- 這碗牛肉麵，我一個人吃得了。
- 這麼多書，我拿不了。
- 颱風來了，今天晚上的喜宴我們去不了了。
- 到現在我還忘不了第一個女朋友。

**Structures:**

**Questions:**

- 還要走半個小時，你走得了走不了？
- 我們點了五個菜，吃得了吃不了？
- 雪這麼大，今天我們到得了山上嗎？

**Usage:** 了 liǎo is an irregular complement. It can be used only in the potential mode, when regular complements can also be used in the actual mode, as indicated below.

| | Actual | Potential |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 了 liǎo | ✗ | V 得了 |
| | | V 不了 |

### VI. Vs + 得 de + complement so … that …

**Function:** This pattern introduces an extent resulting from a state of affairs, "so…that…", in which 'that' corresponds to 得, which brings in a complement.

- 我常常累得起不來，腿也痛得走不了路。
- 天氣冷得很多人不想去上班。
- 夜市裡到處都是人，熱鬧得像過年。

**Structures:**

**Negation:**

- 林教授的演講，複雜得我聽不懂。
- 他去夜市買了很多東西，多得拿不了。
- 他腿痛得跑不完一百公尺。

**Questions:**

- 你是不是累得走不了了？
- 這個房子是不是小得住不了五個人？
- 那家麵包店賣的蛋糕是不是甜得你吃不了？

**Usage:** This pattern is quite compatible with exaggerations, e.g.,

- 她精神差得好像三天沒睡覺。
- 我餓得吃得了一百個小籠包。
- 這個地方好玩得我不想離開。

### VII. To Keep on Doing Something with 下去 xiàqù

**Function:** 下去 is a locative V₁V₂. In this lesson, it is used as a temporal complement to an action verb, with the meaning 'to continue, to keep on'.

- 你說得很好，請你再說下去。
- 我們在夜市逛了很久了，你還要逛下去嗎？
- 雖然學費不便宜，但是我還想念下去。
- 他還沒說完，請大家聽下去。
- 這份工作不錯，雖然薪水少，可是我還要做下去。
- 已經三個小時了，你還要等下去嗎？我想他不會來了。
- 外面這麼吵，你念得下去嗎？

**Structures:** When 下去 combines with transitive verbs, their objects are either moved to the front of the sentence or are omitted if the contexts are clear enough.

- 這個工作，我想一直做下去。
- ＊我想一直做下去這個工作了。
- 雖然很累，我還要念下去。
- ＊雖然很累，我還要念下去中文。

### VIII. Completion of Action with V + 完 wán

**Function:** When the particle 完 follows an action verb, it indicates the completion of the action.

- 他們已經喝完咖啡了。
- 你借給我的書，我都看完了。
- 這個月的薪水，我都用完了。
- 一千公尺我跑得完。

**Structures:**

**Negation:**

- 別買水果了，家裡的還沒吃完呢！
- 我還沒說完，可以再給我一點時間嗎？
- 他工作沒做完，就去花蓮旅行了，所以老闆很不高興。

**Questions:**

- 你是不是考完試了？
- 老師給的功課，你寫完了沒有？
- 那些電影，你看完了沒有？

**Usage:** 完 is a phase marker, which is attached to an action verb. When combining with separable verbs, it is inserted into the verb.

- 我考完試，想去看電影。（＊我考試完，想去看電影。）
- 你們吃完飯，可以去夜市逛逛。
- 我們游完泳，就回家了。

## Grammar Examples in English

### I. 見 jiàn as a Perceptual Result

**Function:**

- Yesterday, I saw Xiaomei at the night market.
- I heard everything that you said.
- Why are you looking for the book? We all saw you take the book back with you.

**Structures:**

**Negation:**

- Where is it? I didn't see it.
- I didn't hear what he said.
- Please say that again. I didn't hear.

**Questions:**

- Did you see my backpack?
- Did you hear what the teacher said?
- What about Mr. Zhang? Did you see him come out?

**Usage:**

- I was not listening to him.
- I didn't hear him talk.

### II. 懂 dǒng as a Cognitive Result

**Function:**

- I understood everything that the teacher said.
- I understood the homework assigned by our teacher after listening to it only once.
- The students could make out all the characters written by the teacher.

**Structures:**

**Negation:**

- What did he say? I didn't understand him.
- I didn't understand that movie the first time I saw it, but I did after the second time.
- The teacher explained for a whole while, but I still didn't understand.

**Questions:**

- Did you understand what he just said?
- Did you understand all this information?
- Did you understand what the teacher was teaching us?

**Usage:**

- (1) Do you now/did you understand what the teacher taught?
- (2) Did you understand (in terms of listening) what the teacher taught?
- (3) Did you understand (in terms of reading) what the teacher taught?

### III. 會 huì as an Achievement Result

**Function:**

- I learned how to drive a car when I was in university.
- It would be difficult to teach students how to write calligraphy (and do well) in just one 1-hour class.
- The students learned how to introduce themselves in Chinese.

**Structures:**

**Negation:**

- I have been learning how to swim for only one week. I can't quite do it yet.
- After two weeks of classes the Master has yet to successfully teach Ma Antong the most difficult Tai Chi move.
- None of us learned how to ride a bike.

**Questions:**

- Have you learned all these characters?
- You've been studying Tai Chi for such a long time and you haven't mastered it yet?
- Has Ma Antong succeeded in teaching those kids to play soccer?

### IV. Potential Capability with -得 de- or -不 bu-

**Function:**

- I catch on to Chinese movies.
- The music played by the garbage truck isn't loud, but I can hear it.
- Of course, I can get up at 8 a.m.
- The movement taught by the master was so easy that anyone could learn it.

**Structures:**

**Negation:**

- I knew him, but I just can't recall his name.
- The night market is too noisy. He didn't hear that I was calling him.
- 3:00 a.m. is too early for anybody to get up.

**Questions:**

- Do you understand the teacher in class?
- The text on that advertisement is so small. Can you even see it?
- Can they learn 100 Chinese characters in only one week?

**Usage:**

- I didn't see that person walk up from the 7th floor.
- I'm on the 10th floor, so I can't see the people on the 7th floor.

### V. Capability Complement 了 liǎo

**Function:**

- I can finish this bowl of beef noodles.
- I cannot carry so many books.
- There is a typhoon coming in so we won't be going to the wedding reception tonight.
- Even now, I still can't forget my first girlfriend.

**Questions:**

- We still need to walk half an hour. Can you make it?
- We ordered five dishes. Will we be able to finish them all?
- With this much snow will we able to reach the mountain today?

### VI. Vs + 得 de + complement so … that …

**Function:**

- I am often so tired that I cannot get up and my legs are hurt so much that I cannot walk.
- The weather was so cold that many people did not want to go to work.
- The night market is full of people. It is as bustling as Lunar New Year.

**Structures:**

**Negation:**

- Professor Lin's speech was so complicated that I was not able to understand.
- He bought a lot of stuff at the night market, so much that he could not carry it all.
- His legs hurt so badly that he couldn't finish running 100 meters.

**Questions:**

- Are you too tired to walk? (or Are you so tired that you can't walk?)
- Is this house too small for five people to live in?
- Are the cakes from that bakery so sweet that you can't even eat them?

**Usage:**

- She has so little energy that she looks as though she hasn't slept for three days.
- I am so hungry that I could eat 100 steamed dumplings.
- This place is so much fun that I don't want to leave.

### VII. To Keep on Doing Something with 下去 xiàqù

**Function:**

- You have spoken well so far. Please continue.
- We've walked the night market for a long time already. Do you want to continue?
- The tuition isn't cheap, but I still want to continue studying.
- He hasn't finished yet. Please let him speak.
- This job isn't bad. Although the pay is low, I want to continue doing it.
- It's been three hours. Do you still want to keep on waiting? I don't think he'll come.
- It's so noisy outside. Can you still study?

**Structures:**

- I want to keep doing this job.
- Although I'm exhausted, I'm still going to keep on studying.

### VIII. Completion of Action with V + 完 wán

**Function:**

- They've already finished drinking their coffees.
- I've finished reading all the books you lent me.
- I have already spent this month's salary.
- I can run 1,000 meters.

**Structures:**

**Negation:**

- Don't buy fruit. We haven't finished the fruit at home yet.
- I haven't finished what I want to say. Can you give me a little more time?
- He took a trip to Hualien before finishing his work, so his boss is very unhappy.

**Questions:**

- Are you finished taking your exams?
- Have you finished the homework that the teacher gave you?
- Did you finish watching those movies?

**Usage:**

- When I finish taking the exam, I'd like to go see a movie.
- When you finish eating, you can go walk around the night market.
- When we finished swimming, we went home.

## 中華文化點滴 Bits of Chinese Culture

### Morning Activities in the Park

In Taiwan, senior citizens and housewives tend to exercise in the mornings. What about all the young people? Due to school and work pressure, few students and people in the labor force have time to exercise in the morning. But seniors tend to be early risers and housewives are free to do as they please after they've finished their housework.

Seniors and housewives gather with friends and neighbors in local parks and plazas, usually before 8 a.m. If you wander by one of these venues at this time, you might see an old man practicing Tai Chi (太極拳) at the foot of a tree or a group of grandmothers doing folk dances on the grass. In another part of the park, you might spot a group of housewives in sportswear doing aerobic dancing.

At 10 a.m. a song blasts out from a park pavilion—the men and women of the local karaoke club are at it again. Nearby, completely unfazed by the racket, two elderly gentlemen play a game of Chinese chess (象棋 xiàngqí), a group of onlookers offering opinions and advice.

As the noon hour approaches, the crowd in the park gradually disperses.
