# 第七課 垃圾車來了

*Dì qī kè — Lèsè chē lái le!*

The Garbage Truck Is Here!

> **Note on pinyin:** Textbook 2 prints no romanization — its text blocks are Traditional + Simplified + English only. The pinyin below is supplied editorially so the lesson can be read aloud; it is not printed in the source.

## 學習目標 Learning Objectives

**Topic: 居家環境 Living Environment**

- Learning to express my views on new events and things.
- Learning to express my understanding of cultural differences.
- Learning to give advice and inform of possible consequences.
- Learning to use directional verbs in expressions.

## 對話 Dialogue

**陳太太：** 東健，要出去啊？

*Chéntàitai: Dōngjiàn, yào chūqù a?*

Mrs. Chen: Dongjian, you're going out?

**東健：** 是啊！垃圾車快來了，我得趕快下樓去倒垃圾。

*Dōngjiàn: Shì a! Lèsè chē kuài lái le, wǒ děi gǎnkuài xià lóu qù dào lèsè.*

Dongjian: Yes. The garbage truck will be here soon. I need to take the garbage out.

**陳太太：** 丟垃圾？今天是星期三，不收垃圾啊！

*Chéntàitai: Diū lèsè? Jīntiān shì xīngqīsān, bù shōu lèsè a!*

Mrs. Chen: Take out the garbage? Today is Wednesday. There is no garbage pickup.

**東健：** 真的嗎？我以為天天都收。

*Dōngjiàn: Zhēn de ma? Wǒ yǐwéi tiāntiān dōu shōu.*

Dongjian: Really? I thought they came every day.

**陳太太：** 不是，每個星期三和星期天是不收的。

*Chéntàitai: Bú shì, měi gè xīngqīsān hàn xīngqītiān shì bù shōu de.*

Mrs. Chen: No. No garbage pickup on Wednesdays or Sundays.

**東健：** 那麼，我這些汽水罐和紙杯也不能拿出去回收了嗎？

*Dōngjiàn: Nàme, wǒ zhèxiē qìshuǐ guàn hàn zhǐbēi yě bù néng ná chūqù huíshōu le ma?*

Dongjian: So, I can't take these soda cans and paper cups of mine out for recycling, either?

**陳太太：** 是啊！

*Chéntàitai: Shì a!*

Mrs. Chen: That's right.

**東健：** 為什麼在台北倒垃圾這麼麻煩？

*Dōngjiàn: Wèishénme zài Táiběi dào lèsè zhème máfan?*

Dongjian: Why is taking out the garbage in Taipei such a pain?

**陳太太：** 剛開始的時候，台北人也覺得這個規定很麻煩，現在大家都習慣了。

*Chéntàitai: Gāng kāishǐ de shíhou, Táiběirén yě juéde zhège guīdìng hěn máfan, xiànzài dàjiā dōu xíguàn le.*

Mrs. Chen: When it first started, the people of Taipei also thought this regulation was a nuisance. Now, everybody is used to it.

**東健：** 更麻煩的是，我一定要在晚上九點把垃圾拿出去，要不然就不能倒了。

*Dōngjiàn: Gèng máfan de shì, wǒ yídìng yào zài wǎnshàng jiǔ diǎn bǎ lèsè ná chūqù, yàoburán jiù bù néng dào le.*

Dongjian: What's worse, I have to take it out at 9 o'clock at night; otherwise, I can't throw it out.

**陳太太：** 你說的對，有一次，我一回來，垃圾車就來了。我馬上放下皮包，跑出去倒垃圾。

*Chéntàitai: Nǐ shuō de duì, yǒu yí cì, wǒ yì huílái, lèsè chē jiù lái le. Wǒ mǎshàng fàngxià píbāo, pǎo chūqù dào lèsè.*

Mrs. Chen: What you say is right. There was one time, the garbage truck arrived just as I was getting home. I immediately put down my purse and ran out to take out the trash.

**東健：** 對啊！我常常看到有人追垃圾車。

*Dōngjiàn: Duì a! Wǒ chángcháng kàndào yǒu rén zhuī lèsè chē.*

Dongjian: Yeah! I often see people chasing the garbage truck.

**陳太太：** 晚上，這麼多人從家裡拿著垃圾走出來，一起站著等垃圾車，你不覺得很有趣嗎？

*Chéntàitai: Wǎnshàng, zhème duō rén cóng jiā lǐ ná zhe lèsè zǒu chūlái, yìqǐ zhàn zhe děng lèsè chē, nǐ bù juéde hěn yǒuqù ma?*

Mrs. Chen: Don't you find it interesting to see so many people at night walking out of their houses with their garbage and then standing around waiting for the garbage truck?

**東健：** 大家常一邊聊天，一邊等，看起來真的很有意思。

*Dōngjiàn: Dàjiā cháng yìbiān liáotiān, yìbiān děng, kàn qǐlái zhēn de hěn yǒu yìsi.*

Dongjian: Everyone likes to chat as they wait. It looks like fun.

**陳太太：** 雖然倒垃圾有點麻煩，但是好像也不壞。

*Chéntàitai: Suīrán dào lèsè yǒudiǎn máfan, dànshì hǎoxiàng yě bú huài.*

Mrs. Chen: Taking out the garbage is somewhat of a nuisance, but it is not all that bad, either.

**東健：** 那麼，我這包垃圾怎麼辦？

*Dōngjiàn: Nàme, wǒ zhè bāo lèsè zěnmebàn?*

Dongjian: So, what do I do with this bag of garbage?

**陳太太：** 你拿回家去，明天再倒吧！

*Chéntàitai: Nǐ ná huí jiā qù, míngtiān zài dào ba!*

Mrs. Chen: Take it home and throw it out tomorrow.

## 文法 Grammar

### I. Directional Constructions V₁V₂ and V₁V₂V₃

**Function:** When a movement is referred to in Chinese, it is necessary to further indicate a direction towards or away from the speaker, the reference point, indicated by V₂. All V₁, V₂ or V₃ are basically verbs themselves. They function differently in combinations.

**Structures:** There are two major directional constructions.

1. Direction + Reference (V₁V₂)

| Direction | Reference |
| --- | --- |
| 上 up | 來 towards me |
| 下 down | 去 away from me |
| 進 into | |
| 出 out of | |
| 回 back to | |
| 過 over | |

- 爸爸出去了。
- 他從火車站東邊的門進去。
- 我的房東還沒回來。

1. Movement + Direction + Reference (V₁V₂V₃)

| Verb | Direction | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 走 | 上 | 來 |
| 跑 | 下 | 去 |
| 站 | 進 | |
| 坐 | 出 | |
| 拿 | 回 | |
| 追 | 過 | |
| 帶 | etc. | |
| 開 | | |
| etc. | | |

- 弟弟從樓下跑上來。
- 我剛看見房東走出去，不知道他要去哪裡。
- 這麼多東西，我怎麼帶回去呢？
- 這個學校，公車可以開進去。

**Negation:**

- 他訂的手機還沒拿回來。
- 我太累了，不走上去了。
- 要考試了，你別跑出去了！

**Questions:**

- 垃圾車就在前面，你為什麼不追過去？
- 我不喜歡臭豆腐，請你拿出去，好嗎？
- 你沒看見公車開過來嗎？

**Placement of a Destination:** The destination of a movement is inserted before the reference, e.g.

- 這些書，請你幫我拿上樓去。
- 在展覽館拿的資料都得帶回美國去。
- 垃圾呢？拿回家去了嗎？
- 這個椅子要搬下樓去嗎？
- 媽媽告訴他錢要記得放進皮包裡去。

**Usage:** When the aspect 了 is used, it is placed after V₁.

- 他從家裡走了出來。
- 公車開了過去。
- 馬安同在夜市看見白如玉，就趕快追了過去。

### II. 把 bǎ with V₁V₂V₃

**Function:** This is a sub-type of the 把-construction, in which a noun has been moved to a new location. This is closely related to Grammar #4 in Lesson 6.

- 我把垃圾拿出去了。
- 李東健把書帶回去了。
- 高美玲從皮包裡把錢拿出來。
- 主任請助教把李小姐的履歷表拿進來。

**Structures:** The basic pattern is Subject + 把 + Obj. + V₁V₂V₃.

**Negation:**

- 我沒把書帶回來。
- 你別把蛋糕放進背包裡去。
- 她沒把買回來的衣服拿出來給朋友看。

**Questions:**

- 張先生是不是把公司的車開回去了？
- 我剛剛請你幫我買一杯咖啡。你把咖啡買回來了嗎？
- 搬家的時候，你要把這些桌子、椅子都搬過去嗎？

**Usage:** The object in the 把-construction is always definite.

- 我把那本英文書帶回來了。
- *我把一本英文書帶回來了。

### III. Confrontation with 嗎 ma

**Function:** When a negative sentence ends with 嗎, the tone is rather impolite and confrontational. The speaker is not asking a question but stating something that should have been known.

- 你不覺得這個漢字很難寫嗎？
- 她不知道今天要考試嗎？
- 馬先生已經在這裡住了兩個月，還不知道丟垃圾的時間嗎？

**Structures:** This pattern is always negative, with either 不 or 沒.

- 你不知道外國人不可以打工嗎？
- 他結婚的事，我沒告訴過你嗎？
- 你沒看見我正在忙嗎？

**Usage:** Confrontational, or rhetorical, questions usually imply the speaker's surprise or annoyance. Such questions are uttered in a high-level intonation, as is also the case in English.

### IV. Existential Sentences with Posture Verbs

**Function:** Posture verbs refer to "sit, stand, lie, kneel, squat etc." They can be used in action sentences or in existential sentences. In the latter we point to the existence of a noun in that physical posture.

- 路口已經站了很多人。
- 活動中心前面站著很多外國學生。
- 咖啡店裡坐著很多來做功課的高中學生。

**Structures:** In existential sentences, posture verbs are typically used with aspect particles (esp. 了 and 著), and the post-verbal subjects are typically indefinite (one, three, many, etc.).

**Questions:**

- 那個展覽館前面是不是站著很多學生？
- 餐廳裡是不是已經坐著很多客人？
- 今天的新郎，是不是在門口站著的那個人？

**Usage:** Non-posture verbs can also occur in this construction, including 放 "place", 走 "walk", 來 "come", 住 "live". In addition, 掛 guà "hang", 躺 tǎng "lie (down)", 貼 tiē "stick", from the following lessons, can also appear in this construction.

### V. 為了 wèile in order to

**Function:** The preposition 為了 refers to "in order to, for the purpose of".

- 為了保護環境，大家都應該做垃圾分類。
- 為了去吃喜酒，高美玲買了一件新衣服。
- 為了早一點回到家，還是坐捷運吧！

**Structures:** The object of 為了 can be either a noun phrase or a verb phrase.

- 他為了父母，買了飛機票回去看他們。
- 馬安同為了搬到便宜的套房去，花了很多時間找房子。

**Negation:** 別 or 不是 negation can be used.

- 別只為了錢去打工。
- 他不是為了錢回收汽水罐，是為了保護環境。
- 你們別為了去旅行就不工作了。

**Questions:**

- 你是不是為了跟同學討論功課，所以晚上沒回家吃飯？
- 白如玉為了什麼到台灣來？
- 你是不是為了幫女朋友照相，所以買了新的照相機？

**Usage:** 為了 is one lexical item. 為了 can be variously translated into English depending on contexts. Extra caution is recommended.

## 中華文化點滴 Bits of Chinese Culture

### Taiwan's Musical Garbage Trucks

Taiwan's garbage trucks have regular routes and times for pickups. How do people know when the trucks are coming? For the past sixty years, they have announced their approach with music. When Taiwanese hear the music, they know it's time to take out the trash. The trucks broadcast either of two light pieces: "La prière d'une vierge" ("A Maiden's Prayer") or Beethoven's "Für Elise." Why classical western music? Some say it's because the then-head of Taiwan's EPA (Environmental Protection Administration) heard his daughter practicing "La prière d'une vierge" on the piano one day. Then and there, he decided to use the simple, lively piece to herald garbage-truck arrivals, later adding "Für Elise" to the repertoire. Garbage pickups in most countries aren't musical, so the practice might seem strange to foreigners and some American students might think it's time for a treat, because ice-cream trucks in the U.S. play music.
