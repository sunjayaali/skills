## 對話 Dialogue 02-01

> （李雲真和白如玉第一次一起去看表演，討論穿著和社交活動）

**李雲真：** 快一點，打扮好了沒有？遲到的話，音樂廳就不讓我們進去，表演恐怕就看不成了。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Kuài yīdiǎn, dǎbàn hǎo le méiyǒu? Chídào de huà, yīnyuètīng jiù bú ràng wǒmen jìnqù, biǎoyǎn kǒngpà jiù kàn bùchéng le.*

**白如玉：** 好的好了，馬上好。妳不認為我們應該穿得正式一點嗎？萬一別人都穿得很正式怎麼辦？

*Bái Rúyù: Hǎo de hǎo le, mǎshàng hǎo. Nǐ bù rènwéi wǒmen yīnggāi chuān de zhèngshì yīdiǎn ma? Wànyī bié rén dōu chuān de hěn zhèngshì zěnme bàn?*

**李雲真：** 我想只要穿得整齊就可以了。我第一次去看表演的時候，以為要穿非常正式的服裝，沒想到平常的穿著就可以了。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Wǒ xiǎng zhǐyào chuān de zhěngqí jiù kěyǐ le. Wǒ dì yīcì qù kàn biǎoyǎn de shíhou, yǐwéi yào chuān fēicháng zhèngshí de fúzhuāng, méi xiǎngdào píngcháng de chuānzhù jiù kěyǐ le.*

**白如玉：** 這是我第一次去，可不能丟臉。

*Bái Rúyù: Zhè shì wǒ dì yīcì qù, kě bù néng diūliǎn.*

**李雲真：** 聽我朋友說，有一次他穿了短褲和涼鞋，就被擋在門外了。我同意妳的看法，去看表演時，穿著整齊對演出者也算是一種尊重。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Tīng wǒ péngyou shuō, yǒu yīcì tā chuān le duǎnkù hé liángxié, jiù bèi dǎng zài mén wài le. Wǒ tóngyì nǐ de kànfǎ, qù kàn biǎoyǎn shí, chuānzhù zhěngqí duì yǎnchūzhě yě suàn shì yī zhǒng zūnzhòng.*

**白如玉：** 每個國家的文化都不太一樣，在我的國家，欣賞藝術表演也算是一種社交活動。

*Bái Rúyù: Mèi gè guójiā de wénhuà dōu bù tài yīyàng, zài wǒ de guójiā, xīnshǎng yìshù biǎoyǎn yě suàn shì yī zhǒng shèjiāo huódòng.*

**李雲真：** 怎麼說呢？

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Zěnme shuō ne?*

**白如玉：** 我們邊走邊說吧，免得遲到就得等中場休息時間才能進去了。準時也是一種禮貌。

*Bái Rúyù: Wǒmen biān zǒu biān shuō ba, miǎndé chídào jiù dé děng zhōngchǎng xiūxí shíjiān cái néng jìnqù le. Zhǔnshí yě shì yī zhǒng lǐmào.*

> （在路上）

**李雲真：** 對了，妳剛說的社交活動是什麼意思？

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Duì le, nǐ gāng shuō de shèjiāo huódòng shì shénme yìsi?*

**白如玉：** 因為我們沒有夜市，KTV也很少，一般人下班後，晚上能去的地方不多，欣賞藝術活動可以跟朋友或同事聯絡感情。

*Bái Rúyù: Yīnwèi wǒmen méiyǒu yèshì, KTV yě hěn shǎo, yībān rén xiàbān hòu, wǎnshang néng qù de dìfang bú duō, xīnshǎng yìshù huódòng kěyǐ gēn péngyou huò tóngshì liánluò gǎnqíng.*

**李雲真：** 說到聯絡感情，後天我和建國要去看電影，一起去吧？

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Shuō dào liánluò gǎnqíng, hòutiān wǒ hé Jiànguó yào qù kàn diànyǐng, yīqǐ qù ba?*

**白如玉：** 妳說的是英文系的林建國嗎？

*Bái Rúyù: Nǐ shuō de shì Yīngwén xì de Lín Jiànguó ma?*

**李雲真：** 就是他，你們很熟嗎？

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Jiùshì tā, nǐmen hěn shú ma?*

**白如玉：** 唉！別提了，上次我們好幾個人一起去看電影，他又摀鼻涕，又不停地咳嗽，還忘了關手機。

*Bái Rúyù: Āi! Bié tí le, shàngcì wǒmen hǎo jǐ gè rén yīqǐ qù kàn diànyǐng, tā yòu wǔ bítì, yòu bù tíng de késòu, hái wàng le guān shǒujī.*

**李雲真：** 哇！怎麼那麼沒有水準，感冒的話就應該在家休息，看電影時手機應該關機。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Wā! Zěnme nàme méiyǒu shuǐzhǔn, gǎnmào de huà jiù yīnggāi zài jiā xiūxi, kàn diànyǐng shí shǒujī yīnggāi guānjī.*

**白如玉：** 到了！到了！還好還有15分鐘才開始。我先去買一本表演手冊。

*Bái Rúyù: Dào le! Dào le! Hái hǎo hái yǒu 15 fēnzhōng cái kāishǐ. Wǒ xiān qù mǎi yī běn biǎoyǎn shǒucè.*

**李雲真：** 我去一下洗手間，等一下裡面見。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Wǒ qù yīxià xǐshǒujiān, děng yīxià lǐmiàn jiàn.*

> （進場後）

**白如玉：** 妳的手機關了嗎？

*Bái Rúyù: Nǐ de shǒujī guān le ma?*

**李雲真：** 謝謝妳提醒我，要不然等一下手機響了就糟了。

*Lǐ Yúnzhēn: Xièxiè nǐ tíxǐng wǒ, yàobùrán děng yīxià shǒujī xiǎng le jiù zāo le.*

**白如玉：** 噓，表演開始了。

*Bái Rúyù: Xū, biǎoyǎn kāishǐ le.*

### English

**Li Yunzhen:** Hurry up, are you done getting ready? If we're late, the concert hall won't let us in and we'll miss the performance.

**Bai Ruyu:** Okay, okay, I'm ready. Don't you think we should dress up a bit? What if everyone else is dressed formally?

**Li Yunzhen:** I think neat is fine. When I first went to a performance, I thought I had to dress very formally. Turns out casual is okay.

**Bai Ruyu:** This is my first time — can't afford to lose face.

**Li Yunzhen:** A friend told me once he wore shorts and sandals and got turned away at the door. I agree with you — dressing neatly is a sign of respect for the performers.

**Bai Ruyu:** Every culture is different. In my country, attending performances is also a social activity.

**Li Yunzhen:** What do you mean?

**Bai Ruyu:** Let's walk and talk so we don't get caught in traffic and have to wait for intermission. Being on time is also polite.

> (On the way)

**Li Yunzhen:** By the way, what did you mean by "social activity"?

**Bai Ruyu:** We don't have night markets, and KTVs are rare. After work, there's not much people do in the evening. Attending arts events lets you bond with friends or colleagues.

**Li Yunzhen:** Speaking of bonding — Jianguo and I are going to a movie the day after tomorrow, want to come?

**Bai Ruyu:** You mean Lin Jianguo from the English department?

**Li Yunzhen:** Yeah, you two close?

**Bai Ruyu:** Ugh, don't get me started. Last time we all went to a movie, he kept sniffing, coughing nonstop, and forgot to turn off his phone.

**Li Yunzhen:** Wow, so rude. If you're sick, you should stay home. And phones should be off during movies.

**Bai Ruyu:** Here! Here! Still 15 minutes to go. Let me grab a program first.

**Li Yunzhen:** I'll use the restroom, meet you inside.

> (After taking seats)

**Bai Ruyu:** Did you turn off your phone?

**Li Yunzhen:** Thanks for reminding me, or it would've rung and ruined everything.

**Bai Ruyu:** Shh, the performance is starting.

## 短文 Reading 02-03

### 享受藝術的生活

今天是白如玉來台灣以後，第一次到「國家戲劇院」看表演，她覺得非常興奮。她跟李雲真約好了，晚上要一起欣賞一場現代舞的表演。在美國時，白如玉曾經看過一次現代舞的表演。她覺得現代舞很難懂，但是很有意思。另外，李雲真還特別強調，今天晚上表演的團體——「雲門舞集」在台灣幾乎無人不知、無人不曉，千萬別錯過了這個難得的好機會。

「國家音樂廳」在「國家戲劇院」對面，在那裡可以欣賞到各種音樂。白如玉小時候學過鋼琴和小提琴，特別喜歡古典音樂，她打算下次要請李雲真一起來聽音樂。白如玉的父親是畫家，她受到父親的影響也非常喜歡畫畫。除了音樂、舞蹈和繪畫以外，她對文學、建築和電影等藝術都非常有興趣。記得剛來台灣時，她常去參觀寺廟，她認為寺廟最能表現出傳統建築之美。她還聽她的同學高橋健太提過，台南有不少古蹟，其中最值得欣賞的就是台南的寺廟了。百聞不如一見，下次一定要去看看。

由於白如玉的中文進步了不少，除了參觀寺廟以外，她還常去看中文電影。上個週末她看了一部台灣電影，劇情非常特別。她想，導演所要表達的意思是現代人孤單寂寞的感覺吧。男、女主角都是台灣目前最紅的明星，他們的演技不但好，男主角還得了今年金馬影展的最佳男主角獎。

白如玉有時逛街經過台北101大樓附近的商業廣場，或週末去淡水老街附近，都看到不少街頭藝術的演出。在我們的生活中仔細留意話，處處都是藝術，捷運站或公園裡也有不少裝置藝術可以欣賞。如果說食物能填飽我們的肚子，滿足我們的口腹之慾的話，那麼藝術就是眼睛和耳朵最豐盛的饗宴了。

### Text in Simplified Characters

#### 享受艺术的生活

今天是白如玉来台湾以后，第一次到「国家戏剧院」看表演，她觉得非常兴奋。她跟李云真约好了，晚上要一起欣赏一场现代舞的表演。在美国时，白如玉曾经看过一次现代舞的表演。她觉得现代舞很难懂，但是很有意思。另外，李云真还特别强调，今天晚上表演的团体——「云门舞集」在台湾几乎无人不知、无人不晓，千万别错过了这个难得的好机会。

「国家音乐厅」在「国家戏剧院」对面，在那里可以欣赏到各种音乐。白如玉小时候学过钢琴和小提琴，特别喜欢古典音乐，她打算下次要请李云真一起来听音乐。白如玉的父亲是画家，她受到父亲的影响也非常喜欢画画。除了音乐、舞蹈和绘画以外，她对文学、建筑和电影等艺术都非常有兴趣。记得刚来台湾时，她常去参观寺庙，她认为寺庙最能表现出传统建筑之美。她还听她的同学高桥健太提过，台南有不少古迹，其中最值得欣赏的就是台南的寺庙了。百闻不如一见，下次一定要去看看。

由于白如玉的中文进步了不少，除了参观寺庙以外，她还常去看中文电影。上个周末她看了一部台湾电影，剧情非常特别。她想，导演所要表达的意思是现代人孤单寂寞的感觉吧。男、女主角都是台湾目前最红的明星，他们的演技不但好，男主角还得了今年金马影展的最佳男主角奖。

白如玉有时逛街经过台北101大楼附近的商业广场，或周末去淡水老街附近，都看到不少街头艺术的演出。在我们的生活中仔细留意的话，处处都是艺术，捷运站或公园里也有不少装置艺术可以欣赏。如果说食物能填饱我们的肚子，满足我们的口腹之欲的话，那么艺术就是眼睛和耳朵最丰盛的飨宴了。

### English

#### A Life Enjoying the Arts

Today is Bai Ruyu's first time at the National Theater in Taiwan since she arrived, and she's very excited. She and Li Yunzhen made plans to see a modern dance performance tonight. Back in the US, Bai Ruyu once saw a modern dance performance before. She found modern dance hard to understand but very interesting. Li Yunzhen also stressed that the performing group tonight — Cloud Gate Dance Theatre — is known to everyone in Taiwan. Don't miss this rare chance.

The National Concert Hall is across from the National Theater, where all kinds of music can be enjoyed. Bai Ruyu learned piano and violin as a child and especially loves classical music; she plans to ask Li Yunzhen to come listen to music next time. Bai Ruyu's father is a painter, and she's also very fond of painting under his influence. Besides music, dance, and painting, she's interested in literature, architecture, and film. When she first came to Taiwan she often visited temples; she thinks temples best represent traditional architecture. She also heard from her classmate Gao Qiao Jianguo that Tainan has many historic sites, the most worth seeing being Tainan's temples. Seeing is believing — she must go next time.

Thanks to Bai Ruyu's improved Chinese, besides visiting temples she often goes to see Chinese-language films. Last weekend she saw a Taiwanese film with a very unusual plot. She thinks the director is trying to express how modern people feel lonely and isolated. The lead actor and actress are the biggest stars in Taiwan now, and the actor won Best Actor at this year's Golden Horse Film Festival.

Sometimes when walking past the commercial plaza near Taipei 101, or on weekends around Danshui Old Street, she sees street art performances everywhere. If you pay attention, art is everywhere — there's installation art to appreciate in metro stations and parks too. If food fills our stomachs and satisfies our appetite, then art is the most sumptuous feast for our eyes and ears.

## 文法 Grammar

### I. Verb Complement 成 successful(ly) — 02-05

**Function:** 成 is a resultative complement referring to the successfulness of an undertaking — "to succeed at the verb."

**Structure:** Subject + Verb + 不 + 成 / 弄 + 成

- 要是你再不努力讀書的話，就念不成醫學系了。
- 如果訂購不成的話，就只好換一個網站訂購了。
- 他又參加了民意代表選舉，可惜這一次他沒連任成。
- 上個月那場演唱會因為颱風而改時間了。等了一個月，今天我終於看成了。
- 高美玲當得成當不成助教，得由系主任來決定。

4. A：你买的票呢？ B：抱歉，我__________________。
5. 問題很複雜，我們商量商量，看看__________________。

### II. 萬一 supposing, in the event of something negative — 02-06

**Function:** This adverb literally means "one in ten thousand" likelihood. It refers to something unlikely but possible nonetheless.

**Structure:** Clause + 萬一 + Clause (undesirable outcome)

- 他請我幫他買一本演出手冊，萬一沒買到，那怎麼辦呢？
- 萬一業務經理知道是我們弄錯的，那就糟了，可能不會再信任我們了。
- 你別太早去，萬一她還沒打扮好，就得在外面等了。
- 多練習幾次吧，萬一我們表演得很糟，就太丟臉了。
- 帶本漫畫去吧，萬一得等很久，就可以看漫畫殺時間。

**Usage:** 萬一 denotes the supposition of an undesirable event, and can also be used in conjunction with other conditional clauses.

1. 就算萬一發生什麼事，也不要緊張。
2. 要是萬一錢不夠的話，你可以去找張先生。

### III. 以為 to have wrongly thought that... — 02-07

**Function:** The verb 以為, especially when used colloquially in conversations, confesses to a mistaken assumption on the part of the speaker or the subject.

**Structure:** Subject + 以為 + clause, 其實 + clause

- 我以為那會是一場美食的饗宴，結果，菜沒什麼特色不說，材料還不新鮮，真是讓人失望。
- 我原來以為你們不熟，沒想到他居然提到了你們小時候常一起玩的事。
- 我們本來以為他會遲到，沒想到他今天很準時。
- 我們都以為這家知名餐廳的服務生一定都很有禮貌，沒想到也有這麼糟的。
- 我們以為這些番茄都是基因改造的，現在才知道這些都是有機番茄。

**Usage:**

1. In formal speech, 以為 and 認為 are used interchangeably freely.
2. 以為 is often used together with 其實 which presents the truth, e.g.,
   (1) 我以為他還單身，其實他已經結婚了。
   (2) 他以為我還住在淡水，其實我早就搬到台中了。

#### 練習 Exercise

### IV. Adverb 可       ◎ 02-08

**Function:** 可, an adverb, has two functions:

1. The speaker's refutation of what people in general expect or assume.
2. A suggestion, mild warning, or reminder.

**Structure:** 可 + 要/不能/別 + Verb

- 你可是男生，怎麼膽子比我還小！
- 我可跟她們不一樣，覺得恐怖片一點也不可怕。
- 最近食品安全出了不少問題，买東西可要小心啊！
- 開慢一點，你可別因為要趕時間而出車禍。

**Usage:** 可 is often used in imperative sentences and is followed by 要, 不能, and 別.

### V. 免得 lest, so as to avoid — 02-09

**Function:** Advice is offered to avoid the occurrence of something undesirable. If what comes before 免得 is properly carried out, what comes after 免得 can be avoided.

**Structure:** Clause + 免得 + Clause (undesirable outcome)

- 你又咳嗽又發燒，快去看醫生吧，免得感冒越來越嚴重。
- 你有空的话去幫一下忙，免得他一個人忙不過來。
- 我們最好注意一下食材的產地，免得買到有問題的食品。

### VI. 曾經 experience in the past, V-ed before — 02-10

**Function:** 曾經 is an adverb that refers to experiences in the past, "to have previously."

**Structure:** Subject + 曾經 + Verb (+ 了) + Object

- 你曾經看過那位導演的電影嗎？
- 媽媽以前曾經抱怨過爸爸每天都早出晚歸。
- 我曾經在一家室內設計公司工作。
- 他的演技很好，曾經得過兩次金馬影展的最佳男主角獎。
- 你曾經在淡水老街看過街頭藝術的演出嗎？

**Usage:**

1. 過 is an experiential aspect, which can be used with 曾經. 過 always appears after the main verb.
2. 曾經 can only be used in affirmative sentences.
3. 曾經 is used in final speech (formal/written).

### VII. Enumeration Particle 等 etc. — 02-11

**Function:** Used in enumeration, 等 may refer to a closed listing or an open-ended listing, depending on the contexts.

**Structure:** Noun + 等 (+ Number + Noun)

- 我去過美國、法國等兩個國家。（closed）
- 我和男朋友的興趣很不一樣，他對文學、繪畫、舞蹈等都沒有興趣。（open）

**Usage:**

1. The total number of listed items can be reiterated following 等.
2. The enumeration particle may be reduplicated (等等).

### VIII. Marking a Verb with the Particle 所 — 02-12

**Function:** The particle 所 marks the verb of a relative clause, which modifies a noun.

**Structure:** 所 + V + 的 + N

- 你上次所提的那個海邊溫泉就是綠島的溫泉吧？
- 這本書所介紹的方法，我早就學過了。
- 政府為了發展觀光所舉辦的活動，吸引了許多人參加。
- 他們所關心的價格問題，其實我們覺得並不重要。
- 我們已經把王先生所訂購的產品寄出了。

**Usage:** The usage of 所 is more formal, and its use is completely optional.

## 中華文化點滴 Bits of Chinese Culture

### 手搖飲料 Taiwan's Hand-Shaken Drinks

Taiwan's unique "hand-shaken drinks" (手搖飲料) culture started long ago in the "iced tea" teahouses that served an earlier variation of the take-out drinks common today (servers in these teahouses would use a cocktail shaker to manually mix tea and ice, producing a cool and frothy drink). Now there are a whole host of these tea-based drinks that can be found in Taiwan, with flavors ranging from the traditional to the more innovative, and almost anything you can think of in between. There are teas of all kinds that cater to consumer's taste buds, with older flavors constantly being replaced by new ones. The Taiwanese have very cleverly transformed tea — traditionally an interest of the elderly — into a multifaceted drink. They have imported cultural bits from all over, mixed and matched in ways that continue to surprise, and ultimately forged a rich and diverse tea culture. Teas from Taiwan have also been marketed the world over, the best example being "Pearl Milk Tea" (珍珠奶茶), which has been a success on the international stage.

Hand-shaken drinks have also become popular because they accord nicely with the Taiwanese way of life — everything from the way teashops are set up and the customized drinks, to the packaging and the way they are consumed. Unlike the Japanese, who are particular about ceremony, and the English, who luxuriate over breakfast tea, Taiwanese tea culture is a thing of whim. No leftover tea leaves, no time wasted on steeping, and no restrictions. You can just take it on the go, wherever and whenever that may be. It is flexible and free and there's no right or wrong way to do it — just the way Taiwanese people like it!
